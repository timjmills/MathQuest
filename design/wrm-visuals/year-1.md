# WRM visual catalogue — Year 1 (US Kindergarten)

Phase 3 of `design/WRM_ALIGNMENT_PLAN.md` for Year 1 (school rule: WRM Year 1 = US grade K): every
representation White Rose Maths draws in the Year 1 small-step materials, how WRM draws it, and whether
our kit can already draw it in our own black-and-white Andika style. **Descriptions only** — no WRM
artwork, wording, characters or layouts are copied into the repo; a match means "our own drawing
carries the same mathematical grammar", never "looks like WRM". Format, legend and citation style
follow the pilot, `design/wrm-visuals/year-3.md`.

- Source: Google Drive, `White Rose Maths Primary / Year 1` (folder `16quj9u1iMa-XhhUxAMsiMCSAGwlSrMaS`),
  14 blocks, **116 small steps** (ids `Y1.B1.S1` … `Y1.B14.S6`, file ids from
  `data/curriculum/wrm-steps.json`). Viewed 2026-09-25.
- For **every** step: the Teaching Guide's "Key model" and "Avoid" lines (read as text; all 116 guides
  are Guide D and all carry a key-model line) **and** the small-step PDF's page 2 (*Key learning*) and
  page 3 (*Reasoning and problem solving*) rendered to PNG and looked at. Coverage is complete: both image
  pages were viewed for all 116 steps (unlike the Year 3 pilot, no uniform runs were skimmed).
- Grade mapping: Year 1 ranges (within 10, 20, 50, 100) are the K / grade 1 bands we already offer
  (`Count to`, `Numbers to`, `Bonds to`); £/p and £ notes map onto our `currency` option (plain / QAR /
  USD) and are not gaps. Several Year 1 steps sit above US K (the v3 crosswalk flags them); this
  catalogue records the visuals whatever the grade.

## Legend

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **MATCH** | An existing kit template / option (or a legacy skill visual that is already B&W-compliant) draws the representation with the same structure. Cite = what to use. |
| **PARTIAL** | We draw the representation, but something WRM relies on differs (task, number range, orientation, what is blank, labelling) — or the drawing exists only as a **support pane** that is not yet wired as a skill option, or only as a **legacy** (coloured, non-kit) visual. What differs is stated. |
| **GAP** | Nothing draws it. A proposal is given: new option on skill X, or new template / skill. |

Citations: `template:<id>` = `js/modules/sheet/cells/<file>.js` registered cell; `pane:<id>` = support
pane in `js/modules/sheet/cells/panes/*.js` (registry `panes/index.js`); `category:skill` = skill key;
option ids are from `js/modules/skill-options.js`. "Y3 Rnn" points at the pilot's de-duplicated table.

**Headline finding.** Year 1 is the year the K-2 kit was built for, and it shows: counting pictures
(`template:counters` kinds count / takeaway / join / tens / teen / sort / share), the ten frame
(`template:tenframe`, `pane:tenframe`), the number bond (`template:bond`, now with a horizontal
orientation), the number track (`template:seqstrip`, box / circle / hexagon tiles), the hundred-chart
window (`template:chartwindow`, charts to 10–100), the two-group compare (`template:compare`), the
+/− hop line (`template:number-line`, with the `ticks` option), the base-10 draw mat (`template:base10`),
the clock and the coins all draw what WRM draws. The Year 1 gaps are of four kinds:
1. **Picture tasks the kit does not ask** — count *out* / ring a subset, match one-to-one with lines,
   share into drawn containers, ring groups of ten, order pictured groups, doubles as a mirrored frame,
   the First–Then–Now three-panel story, the hidden part (hand, cover, bag).
2. **Models that exist only as unwired panes or legacy** — bead string (Y3 R07, still a gap), rekenrek,
   fingers and dice panes (drawn, not offered on the skills that need them), the ten-to-twenty frame
   pair (`tenframe` `frames: 2` is in the kit but `ten_frame_build_teen` still prints the legacy
   format), cube towers (Y3 R41), straw bundles (Y3 R08), bar models (Y3 R25/R26).
3. **Whole K domains with no kit drawing at all** — position and direction (left/right track, grid map
   with a facing arrow, above/below grid, ordinal line, turns), time words (sequence cards, days of the
   week, months and the calendar, the stopwatch), non-standard measures of mass and capacity (balance
   with cubes, glasses at empty…full, capacity in cups), the whole-cm ruler, halves and quarters of a
   shape or a set, 2-D / 3-D shapes and repeating shape patterns in kit B&W.
4. **Number-line reading** at K bands (0–10, 0–20, 0–50, 0–100, end-labelled, estimate on an unmarked
   line) — the same proposed `number_sense:read_number_line` as Y3 R17/R18, with K bands.

## Method notes

Same method as the pilot (`year-3.md` § Method notes), with these Year 1 differences:

- **File ids straight from `data/curriculum/wrm-steps.json`** (branch `claude/sweet-newton-c8wrv1`):
  each step's `drive.lesson` (the 3-page small-step PDF) and `drive.guide` (Guide D). No folder listing
  was needed. Every Year 1 guide is Guide D with a "Key model:" line, so the regex never fell back.
- **Decode by id, not by glob.** The pilot's `decall.py` decodes *every* saved
  `mcp-Google_Drive-download_file_content-*.txt` in the shared tool-results folder, which is unsafe when
  several year agents run at once (one agent's decode can eat another's downloads). The Year 1 decoder
  (`scratchpad/wrm/year-1/dec1.py`) reads each saved file's JSON `id`, keeps only the ids listed for
  Y1 in `wrm-steps.json`, names the output `Y1.Bb.Ss.lesson.pdf` / `.guide.pdf`, and leaves other
  files alone. `tile.py` then tiles pages 2–3 of three steps per 2 × 3 contact sheet at 52 dpi.
- **Losses.** Same-millisecond filename collisions lost 5 downloads out of ≈ 232 (B4 S9, B5 S4, B7 S1,
  B14 S4 lessons, and B8 S3 whose saved file vanished — most likely consumed by a parallel agent's
  glob decoder); each was re-downloaded singly and viewed. Check the decoded count after every batch.
- **Cost.** 116 steps: ≈ 16 batches of 13–20 parallel downloads (guides ≈ 280 KB, lessons 1–4 MB),
  21 contact sheets read. Downloads were deleted after each block; disk never passed ≈ 60 MB.
- **What the WRM pages lean on in Year 1** (for the build lanes): concrete objects in a line, ten frames
  (one, then two), part-whole, the First–Then–Now story, number tracks *before* number lines (the
  guides say so explicitly: B1 S6 avoids the line; B6 S6 contrasts them), base 10 only from B4, cubes as
  the non-standard unit for length, mass and capacity alike, and no × / ÷ / fraction notation (B9, B10
  guides avoid the symbols) — so the Y1 pages of our multiplication, division and fraction skills must
  be able to stop at words and pictures.
