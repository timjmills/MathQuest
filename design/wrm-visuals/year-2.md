# WRM visual catalogue — Year 2 (US Grade 1)

Phase 3 of `design/WRM_ALIGNMENT_PLAN.md`, same method and format as the Year 3 pilot
(`design/wrm-visuals/year-3.md`): every representation White Rose Maths draws in the Year 2 small-step
materials, how WRM draws it, and whether our kit can already draw it in our own black-and-white Andika
style. **Descriptions only** — no WRM artwork, wording or layouts are copied into the repo; a match means
"our own drawing carries the same mathematical grammar", never "looks like WRM".

- Source: Google Drive, `White Rose Maths Primary / Year 2` (folder `1QxPJQV5ZCU5VUXjuRSzuGowQmypjZVfN`),
  11 blocks, **124 small steps** (`Y2.B1.S1` … `Y2.B11.S5`; file ids from `data/curriculum/wrm-steps.json`).
  Viewed 2026-09-25. Year 2 = US Grade 1 (school rule); UK £/p and UK coins map onto our `currency` option.
- For every step: the Teaching Guide's "Key model" and "Avoid" lines (read as text, all 124 guides) **and**
  the small-step PDF pages 2 (*Key learning*) and 3 (*Reasoning and problem solving*), rendered and looked
  at. Coverage honesty: both pages were viewed for **every** step except Block 3 (Shape), where all 12
  key-learning pages were viewed and the reasoning page only for S2, S4, S6, S7, S11, S12. Teaching decks
  and worksheets were not opened.
- Where a guide names a model the two pages do not show (e.g. B1 S9 "line to 10 above line to 100",
  B1 S15 "1–50 grid", B9 S2 "quarter shaded + past/to arc", B9 S7 "midnight → midnight day bar") the row
  says "(guide)".

## Legend

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **MATCH** | An existing kit template / option (or a legacy skill visual that is already B&W-compliant) draws the representation with the same structure. Cite = what to use. |
| **PARTIAL** | We draw the representation, but something WRM relies on differs (task, number range, orientation, what is blank, labelling) — or the drawing exists only as a **support pane** that is not yet wired as a skill option, or only as a **legacy** (coloured, non-kit) visual. What differs is stated. |
| **GAP** | Nothing draws it. A proposal is given: new option on skill X, or new template / skill. |

Citations: `template:<id>` = `js/modules/sheet/cells/<file>.js` registered cell; `pane:<id>` = support
pane in `js/modules/sheet/cells/panes/*.js` (registry `panes/index.js`); `category:skill` = skill key;
option ids are from `js/modules/skill-options.js`. `R`-numbers are the de-duplicated representation ids of
the Year 3 table (R01–R84); representations **new in Year 2** continue the numbering from **R85**.

**Headline finding.** Year 2 is a *picture* year: almost every step is taught through base-10, ten frames,
the rekenrek, equal-groups pictures, bar models with counters in the parts, and real-object measuring
instruments — and **Year 2 never uses a column method** (addition and subtraction of 2-digit numbers are
drawn as base-10 in two stacked boxes, one number per box). Three things changed in the kit since the Year 3
pilot and lift Year 2 rows: the **`pane:rekenrek`**, **`pane:dice`** and **`pane:fingers`** K–2 panes exist
(still not wired as `support` values); the **B&W fraction model** `frac-model.js` (area / bar / circle / line)
is offered through the `model` option on five fraction skills; and the `bond` payload accepts
`orientation: 'horizontal'`. The real GAPs cluster in **measurement instruments** (cm ruler, dial, jugs and
barrels, thermometer read/colour — the same instrument family as Year 3), **statistics** (block diagrams,
blank tally charts, keyed pictograms in B&W), **position and direction** (the whole of B11: position grids,
moves on a grid, turn diagrams — none exists), **shape work on a grid** (draw on dotty/squared paper,
complete a symmetric half), and a few **number pictures** (100-bead rekenrek, number shapes, covered parts,
repeating shape patterns).

## Method notes (Year 2 run)

**Files.** `data/curriculum/wrm-steps.json` gives each step's `drive.lesson` (the 3-page small-step PDF,
1.1–2.6 MB; B4 money steps are 2–8 MB because of the coin photographs, all under the 10 MB cap) and
`drive.guide` (Teaching Guide, ≈ 280 KB). No folder listing was needed. Pages 2 and 3 of the small-step PDF
were rendered at 52 dpi and tiled 2 × 3 (three steps per image; six key-learning pages per image for Block 3).

**Parallel-agent safety.** Several year agents share one `tool-results` directory, so the pilot's
`decall.py` (which decodes *every* saved download and deletes it) would steal other agents' files. This run
used `wrm/year-2/dec2.py`: it decodes **only** saved results whose JSON `id` is one of this year's lesson or
guide ids, names each file by step id (`Y2.B5.S8.L.pdf`), and extracts the guide lines. Reuse it for the other
years by changing the manifest.

**Pitfalls seen.** The same-millisecond collision happened **five times** in 124 × 2 downloads (B2 S8 lesson,
B5 S12/S14 guides, B5 S6 lesson, B7 S1 guide / B6 S2 lesson, B8 S8 and S11 lessons, B10 S6 lesson): check the
decoded list against the batch and re-download the missing ids. Guide text for Year 2 is uniformly Guide D
(every guide had a "Key model" line). Year 2 names models the pages sometimes do not show (double number line,
1–50 grid, past / to arc, day bar) — recorded as "(guide)".
