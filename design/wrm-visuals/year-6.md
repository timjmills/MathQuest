# WRM visual catalogue — Year 6 (US Grade 5)

Phase 3 of `design/WRM_ALIGNMENT_PLAN.md` for Year 6 (school rule: WRM Year 6 = US grade 5; `usGrade: '5'`
in `data/curriculum/wrm-steps.json`): every representation White Rose Maths draws in the Year 6 small-step
materials, how WRM draws it, and whether our kit can already draw it in our own black-and-white Andika style.
**Descriptions only** — no WRM artwork, wording, characters or layouts are copied into the repo; a match means
"our own drawing carries the same mathematical grammar", never "looks like WRM". Format, legend and citation
style follow the pilot, `design/wrm-visuals/year-3.md`.

- Source: Google Drive, `White Rose Maths Primary / Year 6` (folder `1ZJc-MlhbDHAFCNn_QWw1ZsLPg3ZRwqBs`),
  13 blocks, **114 small steps** (`Y6.B1.S1` … `Y6.B13.S5`; file ids from `data/curriculum/wrm-steps.json`).
  The two other folders (`14 Year 6 Projects`, `15 Taskmaster`) have no small steps and were not catalogued.
  Viewed 2026-09-25.
- For **every** step: the Teaching Guide's "Key model" and "Avoid" lines (read as text; all 114 guides are
  Guide D and all carry a key-model line — `Y6.B4.S7` lists two guide ids, the first was read) **and** the
  small-step PDF's page 2 (*Key learning*) and page 3 (*Reasoning and problem solving*) rendered to PNG and
  looked at. Coverage is complete: both image pages were viewed for all 114 steps. Teaching decks and
  worksheets were not opened. Where a guide names a model the two pages do not show, the row says "(guide)".
- Grade mapping: UK £/p map onto our `currency` option; UK imperial units (stone, pint, gallon) are part of
  the step's content (B5 S5), not a currency question. Several Year 6 steps sit in US grade 6 (ratio, percent,
  four-quadrant coordinates, area of triangles and parallelograms, the mean); the catalogue records the
  visuals whatever the grade.

## Legend

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **MATCH** | An existing kit template / option (or a legacy skill visual that is already B&W-compliant) draws the representation with the same structure. Cite = what to use. |
| **PARTIAL** | We draw the representation, but something WRM relies on differs (task, number range, orientation, what is blank, labelling) — or the drawing exists only as a **support pane** that is not yet wired as a skill option, or only as a **legacy** (coloured, non-kit) visual. What differs is stated. |
| **GAP** | Nothing draws it. A proposal is given: new option on skill X, or new template / skill. |

Citations: `template:<id>` = `js/modules/sheet/cells/<file>.js` registered cell; `pane:<id>` = support
pane in `js/modules/sheet/cells/panes/*.js` (registry `panes/index.js`); `category:skill` = skill key;
option ids are from `js/modules/skill-options.js`. `Rnn` = the Year 3 pilot's de-duplicated ids (R01–R84);
`Y2 Rnn` = Year 2's additions (R85–R101); `Y1 Rnn` = Year 1's own table; **`Nnn` = new in Year 6** (numbered
locally so parallel year lanes cannot collide). "PROP x" = the proposal already listed for that step in
`WRM_PROPOSALS` (`js/modules/wrm.js`); "legacy (C)" = the skill's print format is flagged for colour in
`design/SKILL_CATALOGUE.md`.

**Headline finding.** Year 6 is the year the kit is thinnest. The representations WRM leans on are
(1) the **place-value chart with counters** stretched to 10,000,000 and down to thousandths, plus the
**Gattegno chart**; (2) the **bar model in every guise** — fraction bars and walls, equal-parts bars for
fractions and percentages of amounts, ratio bars, algebra bars with letters, 180° angle bars; (3) the **double
number line and operator arcs** for multiplicative reasoning (ratio, proportion, unit conversion, equivalent
fractions and percentages); (4) **formal written methods on squared paper** (column add/subtract to 7 digits and
with decimals, long multiplication, short "bus stop" division, long division with a multiples list) with the
PV-counter exchange drawn beside; (5) **geometry diagrams** (missing-angle diagrams, protractor, circle parts,
nets, constructions, triangles and parallelograms on squared grids, cube builds) and **coordinate grids** in one
and four quadrants with translations and reflections; (6) **statistics** — line graphs, dual bar charts, pie
charts read and drawn, the mean as levelling. What already matches is a short list: `template:pv` (whole
numbers, letters to M, but skill bands stop at 999,999), the `place_value_10x` shift chart, `template:division`
(the long-division bracket), `template:stack` (column add/subtract of whole numbers), `template:function-table`,
the B&W `frac-model` on seven fraction skills, `template:bond`, `template:arrays` and the compare circle. Almost
everything else is either a **legacy coloured visual** (fraction operations beyond like denominators, decimal
operations, percent grid, double number line, area/volume, angles, coordinates, transformations, pie chart,
mean) or a **GAP** (decimal PV chart, Gattegno chart, operator arcs, long multiplication and short division
layouts, factor tree, percentage bars, ratio bars, algebra cubes, line graphs, missing-angle diagrams, circle
parts, scale drawing).

## Method notes (Year 6 run)

Same method as the pilot (`year-3.md` § Method notes) and the Year 1 / Year 2 runs, with these differences:

- **Downloads by `curl`, not the Drive connector.** The files are shared "anyone with the link", so
  `curl -sL "https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=<id>"` fetched each PDF straight to disk, eight in
  parallel (`scratchpad/wrm/year-6/dl.py`: per block, guides + lessons, named `Y6.Bb.Ss.G.pdf` / `.L.pdf`, each
  checked for a `%PDF-` header and retried). Nothing passed through the harness's shared `tool-results`
  folder, so the parallel-agent decode clash and the same-millisecond filename collision of earlier runs could
  not happen: **zero losses in 228 downloads**; each block of 30–50 files took about 20 s.
- **File ids from `data/curriculum/wrm-steps.json`** (branch `claude/sweet-newton-c8wrv1`); `Y6.B4.S7` lists two
  guide ids (the first was used) — a script that assumes a string id fails on it.
- **Guides**: every Year 6 guide is Guide D with a "Key model:" line; `guides.py` extracts the key-model and
  "Avoid" lines locally with PyMuPDF (≈ 80 tokens per guide).
- **Pages**: `tile.py` renders pages 2–3 of three steps per 2 × 3 contact sheet at 52 dpi (1218 × 1290 px); 42
  sheets covered all 114 steps on both pages. Year 6 pages are denser than Year 1–3 (7-digit numbers, fraction
  labels, degree labels) but stayed legible at 52 dpi. Downloads were deleted after each block (disk stayed
  under 30 MB).
- **Kit cross-check** used the live tree: `design/SKILL_CATALOGUE.md` (print formats and the colour flag),
  `js/modules/skill-options.js` (bands, `model`, `support`, `ticks`), `js/modules/sheet/cells/*` and
  `js/modules/wrm.js` (`SKILL_WRM` and `WRM_PROPOSALS` for each Y6 step, cited as "PROP").
- **What the WRM pages lean on in Year 6** (for the build lanes): the PV chart with counters (now also below 1)
  and the Gattegno chart for every × / ÷ by 10; the bar model for fractions, percentages, ratio, algebra and
  angle facts; the double number line and operator arcs for anything multiplicative; formal methods always on
  squared paper with the counter picture beside; and in geometry, diagrams to *calculate* from (marked with
  arcs, ticks and right-angle squares) rather than to measure. The guides repeatedly say "avoid the rule
  before the model" (no "add a zero", no BODMAS rule, no ½ × b × h before counting squares) — so the Year 6
  pages of our skills need the picture option on, not only the equation.
