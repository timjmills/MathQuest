# WRM visual catalogue — Year 4 (US Grade 3)

Phase 3 of `design/WRM_ALIGNMENT_PLAN.md` for Year 4 (school rule: WRM Year 4 = US grade 3): every
representation White Rose Maths draws in the Year 4 small-step materials, how WRM draws it, and whether
our kit can already draw it in our own black-and-white Andika style. **Descriptions only** — no WRM
artwork, wording, characters or layouts are copied into the repo; a match means "our own drawing
carries the same mathematical grammar", never "looks like WRM". Format, legend and citation style
follow the pilot, `design/wrm-visuals/year-3.md`, and the Year 1 catalogue, `design/wrm-visuals/year-1.md`.

- Source: Google Drive, `White Rose Maths Primary / Year 4` (folder `1WoeDn6QNmbDFeReN50Hk1ekEsht_Sgoi`),
  14 blocks, **129 small steps** (ids `Y4.B1.S1` … `Y4.B14.S5`, file ids from
  `data/curriculum/wrm-steps.json`). Viewed 2026-09-25.
- For **every** step: the Teaching Guide's "Key model" and "Avoid" lines (read as text; all 129 guides
  carry a key-model line, some in upper case) **and** the small-step PDF's page 2 (*Key learning*) and
  page 3 (*Reasoning and problem solving*) rendered to PNG and looked at. Coverage is complete: both
  image pages were viewed for all 129 steps.
- Grade mapping: WRM Year 4 runs to 10,000 and into decimals, improper fractions, the 24-hour clock,
  line graphs and coordinates — much of it above US grade 3 (the v3 crosswalk flags it; it relocates to
  grade 4 / 5 standards). This catalogue records the visuals whatever the grade. £/p maps onto our
  `currency` option (plain / QAR / USD), km / m / cm onto our metric units; neither is a gap.

## Legend

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **MATCH** | An existing kit template / option (or a legacy skill visual that is already B&W-compliant) draws the representation with the same structure. Cite = what to use. |
| **PARTIAL** | We draw the representation, but something WRM relies on differs (task, number range, orientation, what is blank, labelling) — or the drawing exists only as a **support pane** that is not yet wired as a skill option, or only as a **legacy** (coloured, non-kit) visual. What differs is stated. |
| **GAP** | Nothing draws it. A proposal is given: new option on skill X, or new template / skill. |

Citations: `template:<id>` = `js/modules/sheet/cells/<file>.js` registered cell; `pane:<id>` = support
pane in `js/modules/sheet/cells/panes/*.js` (registry `panes/index.js`); `category:skill` = skill key;
option ids are from `js/modules/skill-options.js`. "Y3 Rnn" / "Y1 Rnn" point at those years'
de-duplicated tables.

**Headline finding.** Year 4 reuses the Year 3 number grammar at a bigger scale (to 10,000) and the
kit keeps up where Year 3 already matched: the `stack` column (4-digit add / subtract with exchanges,
short multiplication), `pv` disks / build / compare / order / round (places to thousands), the rounding
line and rounding table, `count-row` tracks in any step, `arrays`, `mult-grid`, `bond`, `clock`,
`timeline`, `coins`, the perimeter grid. The Year 4 gaps come in five clusters:
1. **Decimals have no kit drawing at all** (Blocks 8–9, 18 steps): a Ones · Tenths · Hundredths place-value
   chart with a decimal point, decimal counters (0.1 / 0.01), the hundred square as one whole, the
   Gattegno chart, a rekenrek of 100, decimal number tracks and lines. The fraction `frac-model` (bar,
   line) draws tenths only as fractions.
2. **Place-value movement pictures** — two stacked charts with a ×10 / ÷10 / ×100 / ÷100 arrow, the
   exchange ring-and-arrow beside a column (Y3 R31, still open), sharing PV counters into rows for
   division, and the split-and-operate part-whole (Y3 R15, still open).
3. **Fractions beyond one** — mixed-number pictures across several wholes, fraction lines past 1 with
   hops, double lines (mixed over improper, fraction over decimal), fraction tracks, fraction counters
   ringed into wholes; all still legacy or absent (Y3 R53–R57 had the 0–1 rung).
4. **New Year 4 domains** — area by counting squares (incl. half squares), regular-polygon perimeter
   with hatch marks, Roman numerals to C, the 24-hour clock, **line graphs**, the **first-quadrant
   coordinate grid** with translations, and completing a figure across a diagonal mirror line.
5. **The shared table family** (Y3 R58 / R59, Y1 R46): conversion tables, sort tables, rounding and
   price tables, the combinations grid and the representation table (Picture | Words | Fraction |
   Decimal) — Year 4 uses them in 20+ steps.

## Method notes

Same method as the pilot (`year-3.md` § Method notes) and Year 1, with these Year 4 differences:

- **File ids straight from `data/curriculum/wrm-steps.json`** (branch `claude/sweet-newton-c8wrv1`):
  each step's `drive.lesson` (the 3-page small-step PDF) and `drive.guide`. No folder listing was
  needed. Every Year 4 guide carries a key-model line ("Key model:" or upper-case "KEY MODEL"), so the
  case-insensitive regex never needed the keyword fallback.
- **Downloaded with `curl`, not the Drive tool.** The files are shared "anyone with the link", so
  `curl -sL "https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=<id>" -o <step>.pdf` fetches each PDF
  straight to disk without passing base64 through the conversation. A small script
  (`scratchpad/wrm/year-4/get.py <block>`) pulls one block's lessons and guides eight at a time,
  names them `Y4.Bb.Ss.lesson.pdf` / `.guide.pdf`, checks each starts with `%PDF-` and retries. This
  removes both earlier pitfalls at once: no same-millisecond saved-result collisions and no shared
  tool-results folder for parallel year agents to steal from. **Zero downloads were lost** (258 / 258).
- `guides.py <block>` prints only the key-model and "Avoid" lines per guide; `tile.py` tiles pages 2–3
  of three steps into one 2 × 3 contact sheet at 52 dpi (PyMuPDF), as in the pilot. 46 contact sheets
  were read. PDFs were deleted after each block (disk never passed ≈ 70 MB).
- **What the WRM pages lean on in Year 4** (for the build lanes): the place-value chart is the spine
  of every number block — valued counters, then **plain dots**, then digits, always **beside** the
  written method, never instead of it; exchanges are drawn as a ring with an arrow; ×10 / ÷10 is taught
  as digits moving between two stacked charts ("never add a zero"); division is **sharing counters into
  rows** of a chart plus a split-and-divide part-whole, with no bus-stop notation in Year 4 (so our US
  bracket `template:division` is not a Year 4 model); the number line (count intervals, midpoint, then
  quarters) carries rounding, estimation, fractions, decimals and money alike; fractions past one are
  one bar per whole; decimals are introduced through tenths pictures before the chart, and the hundred
  square is "one whole", not "a hundred".
