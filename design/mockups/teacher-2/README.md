**Fix round 2 (2026-10-23).** The two defects that survived two critic rounds are now machine checks: `shoot.cjs` fails on
any text cut off (ellipsis included), a chip name under ~8 characters or ellipsized while its row has room, a child
spilling past its chip / card / box, a title broken inside a word, a control under 44 px, the tick box on a drawing,
Skills at 820–1099 with a narrow search or cards under 200 px, a doc page that scrolls sideways, and a missing grade
badge — at all five widths, light and dark. `ws-teacher-tokens.cjs` adds checks 10–13: bolds clearly not the ink or
cream primary, dark tints ≥ 1.2 : 1 and bolds ≥ 3 : 1 on the dark page, every tint ≥ 3 CIEDE2000 from what it sits on,
and the grade badge in neutral tokens only. `thumbs-check.cjs` holds Visual / Pictures variants to their pictures.
Quick runs while fixing: `node shoot.cjs --only skills-tray,chip --widths 820,1024 --themes light --no-flows`.

- **Grade badge (owner):** grade is a numbered ink circle (K, 1 … 6), never an area colour, always the first thing in a
  skill card, chip, lesson row, lesson panel and chip-options header. A search across grades and Mixed levels group each
  area's skills by grade.
- **Warm stone (owner):** the light page is #EFECE8 with white surfaces; cream stays only as text on dark fills. Area
  groups are white cards with the tint inside, so every tint shows.
