Every skill’s thumbnail

Each live skill with the picture its card shows, grouped by category. Every picture is a real item: node capture-samples.cjs prints a seeded sheet of each skill through the real app (the sheet kit and generateQuestionFor, the skill’s default options) and keeps its first cells exactly as they print, cropped to what is drawn. node thumbs-check.cjs holds every thumbnail to its own skill’s captured item.
