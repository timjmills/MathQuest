Every skill’s thumbnail

Each live skill with the picture its card shows, grouped by category. Nothing here is copied: every picture is drawn live by the app as you scroll (live.mjs imports the app’s buildSheet read-only, prints the skill’s seeded sheet with its default options and shows its first cell in the app’s own sheet styles). node thumbs-check.cjs holds every thumbnail to the booted app’s own sheet.
