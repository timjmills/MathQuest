# Family review: measurement-data

Categories: `measurement` (39), `graphs` (10), `data_analysis` (9), `probability` (2), `data_mixed` (1). 61 skills. Generators: `js/modules/gen-measurement.js` (measurement, plus `pictograph_intro` :736, `bar_graph_intro` :809, `heavier_lighter_visual` :690) and `js/modules/gen-data-stats.js` (graphs, data analysis, probability). Three skills in `measurement` belong to the geometry family's ladders and are reviewed there (`order_objects_length`, `measure_nonstandard`, `perimeter_intro` live in `gen-measurement.js` but are catalogued under `shapes_early`). Machine-readable tags and verdicts: `design/catalogue/measurement-data.overrides.json`.

## Family summary

1. Verdicts: keep 5, fix 21, redo 16, split 18, merge 1 (total 61). Hosts: `visual-grid` 50, `equation-drill` 8, `computation-grid` 1 (`money`), `chart-table` 1 (`tally_chart`), `word-problems` 1 (`unit_conversion_word`). The catalogue's `unassigned` guesses (6) and its `equation-drill` guesses for `time_5min`, `mixed_time`, `make_change_least_coins` are all wrong — those skills carry a picture.
2. Worst problem 1: **broken or unanswerable print.** `print-settings.js:1401-1420` copies a fixed field list from the generated question and drops `q.tiles`, `q.bins`, `q.dndMode`, `q.orderLabel`, `q.minuend`, `q.subtrahend`, `q.operands`, `q.colMode`, `q.targetData`, `q.weightData` and `q.conversionWordData`. Every drag item in this family therefore prints as a bare instruction with one ruled line and no content: all four `order_clocks_*` (baseline `measurement__order_clocks_analog_asc.png`, six identical "Drag the analog clocks in order from EARLIEST to LATEST." rows), and the sort branches of `temperature`, `capacity`, `estimate_length`, `mass_volume_liquid`, `time_analog_digital` and `probability_basic`. Separately, `print-generate.js:10642` reads `dd.values` for pie charts while `gen-data-stats.js:~1290` writes `dd.percents`, so every `pie_chart` item prints an empty circle captioned "Jan: undefined | Feb: undefined" (baseline `graphs__pie_chart.png`). `print-generate.js:10714-10726` draws a thermometer scaled `temp/120` against a 0/50/100 scale, so a 7 °C reading is invisible and a negative reading gives a negative rect height (baseline `measurement__temperature.png`, items 2-4 are unanswerable). `measurement-clock-match` (:10697) prints only the **correct** clock for a "Which clock shows …?" item, so `time_match_clock` and half of `time_analog_digital` print the answer and nothing to choose between (baseline `measurement__time_match_clock.png`).
3. Worst problem 2: **the answer is printed on the item.** `bar_graph` writes each bar's value above the bar (`gen-data-stats.js:~885`), so "How many chose Bananas?" is reading a numeral, not reading a scale; the multi-select branches of `bar_graph`, `pictograph` and `tally_chart` label every option "Cats (9)". `tally_chart`'s screen table has a Count column holding the number (:~1190) — the print path drops it, so paper and screen are different tasks. `pie_chart` prints the percent inside every sector **and** in the legend. `temperature`'s read mode has no thermometer at all: the visual is the temperature itself in 2 rem coloured type (`gen-measurement.js:2199-2203`). Hints give the answer outright in `make_change_least_coins` (:2845, the whole greedy decomposition), `mad` (:107, the finished MAD), `enough_money` (:2907, the coin total), `probability_basic` (:1568, the fraction) and `mean` (:640, the sum).
4. Worst problem 3: **silent type mixing (P-28) in 30 of 61 skills.** Every `time_*` and `elapsed_*` skill has an unannounced 30% "Set the clock" branch that replaces a reading item with a hand-dragging item (:1249, :1286, :1329, :1377, :1416, :1705, :1762, :1819, :1875, :1966). `elapsed_30min`/`_hour`/`_15min` also flip forward/backward per item. Every data skill rotates 3-5 question kinds (`bar_graph` :~860 five kinds, `histogram_read` :257 five, `box_plot_intro` :159 seven, `pictograph`, `tally_chart`, `pie_chart`, `line_plot`, `line_plot_g2`, `line_plot_fractions` three each) and most carry an extra 20-25% multi-select or drag branch on top. `estimate_length` mixes measurement systems: the main branch is metric (:305-315) while its two variants are customary. `capacity` (:2778) and `unit_conversions` (:415) mix metric and customary in one pool, and `unit_conversion_word` (:991-1003) mixes length, weight, volume and time, one-step and two-step.
5. Worst problem 4: **a written answer becomes multiple choice on screen (P-29).** `question-render.js:4945` makes any question with a non-empty `q.options` a four-button choice, whatever its `answerType`. In this family that silently converts write-in items in `reading_ruler`, `reading_ruler_hard` (12 of 12 items each), every `time_*` and `elapsed_*` reading item, `estimate_length`, `unit_conversions`, `capacity`, `temperature`, `mean`, `mode`, `range`, `bar_graph`, `pictograph`, `tally_chart`, `line_plot`, `line_plot_fractions` and `pie_chart`. `box_plot_intro` is worse: `gen-data-stats.js:212` tosses a coin per item between a number input and a four-option choice. `make_change_least_coins` is the mirror image — the screen is a live coin-builder, the worksheet path rewrites the item as "these coins make 47¢, how many coins is that?" with the coins already laid out (:2854-2875).
6. Worst problem 5: **colour and emoji carry meaning.** Emoji are the content in `heavier_lighter_visual` (:690, 🪶🍃🍎📕🐕🚗🚛 as the objects to compare), `pictograph_intro` (:737, animal and fruit emoji as graph icons), `build_pictograph` (:370), `capacity` (:2705), and `money_count`'s combination options (:2410, 🪙). `unit_conversion_word` prints a parchment-gradient "Key" card with ⚖️🧪📏⏱️ and the whole conversion table on every item (:1084-1098). Colour is the only key in `pie_chart` (slice fill ↔ legend swatch), `probability_basic` (outcomes are named by colour words and drawn in colour, :1541-1562, against playbook rule PR-R1), the generic coin renderer (:2799-2805 gives 1/5/10/25/100 brown/silver/gold fills — the owner's ruling is sized by value, not coloured), `temperature`'s reading (blue/green/red by value), `line_plot_g2`'s blue × marks, `box_plot_intro`, `histogram_read` and every clock (`colorScheme` picked at random at :1136, purple hands in the printed baseline).
7. Also family-wide: a purple screen-only title div sits above nearly every measurement visual and repeats the skill label; prompts use capitals for emphasis ("Click ALL", "EARLIEST to LATEST", "How many MORE", "STATISTICAL"); `mean`, `median`, `mode`, `range`, `bar_graph`, `pictograph`, `tally_chart`, `pie_chart` and `probability_basic` print the CCSS code on the **pupil** page, against PT-DOC-5, and several of the codes are wrong (mean/median/mode tagged 5.MD.B.2, probability tagged 4.MD.B.4); `STUDENT_DEF_*` definition boxes and step lists print on practice items in `mean`, `median`, `mode`, `range`, `box_plot_intro` and `bar_graph`; `probability_basic`'s printed scaffold box gives the total outcomes away in orange fill.
8. `state.range` is ignored by most of the family. `length_customary` :51 and `length_metric` :72 use `randInt(2, 12)` regardless of range and include miles→feet (5280 × 12 = 63,360) and m→mm at grade 4; `unit_conversions` uses its own `maxFrom` per pair; every `time_*`, `elapsed_*`, `temperature`, `capacity`, `mean`, `median`, `mode`, `range`, `mad`, `box_plot_intro`, `histogram_read` and `probability_basic` is fixed-domain. Only `money_count` (:2554 bills capped by range), `make_change_least_coins` (:2834) and the `dataMax` used by `bar_graph`/`pictograph`/`mean`/`median`/`mode`/`range` (:630) read it at all, and `dataMax` scales the *data values*, which is the wrong axis: at range 1000 a grade 3 bar graph asks for the difference between 47 and 12.
9. What is already good: the ruler SVG (:1253-1290) is a true tick hierarchy in black and white with no colour; `money_count`'s coin and bill renderers (:2385-2400) are already black-outline value circles sized by value and match the owner's ruling; the box plot, histogram and line-plot-with-fractions print cells render correctly and legibly; `mad`'s ten datasets are chosen so the answer is clean; `bar_graph_intro` uses a single bar colour and a labelled 0-5 axis, which is the right axis design; the elapsed print cell (`print-generate.js:10678`) correctly shows the start clock only; `statistical_question`'s bank pairs each statistical question with its own non-statistical twin, which is exactly the contrast a Reason It page needs.
10. Worked text: `workedMax` is 5-7 for the time and elapsed skills (the hint restated) and 0-3 elsewhere; `mean`, `mad` and `box_plot_intro` have multi-sentence hints that are the worked solution, so they are usable as the **key line** but not as `workedSteps`. `workedSteps`, `wrongAnswer`, the `Say:` frame and vocabulary must be authored for all 56 non-mixed skills. The playbook already supplies the misconception sets ME-M1..M7, TC-M1..M7, GD-M1..M9, PR-M1..M7; the time and money ladders have no misconception set and one is proposed below.
