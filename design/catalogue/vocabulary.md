# Family review: vocabulary

Category: `vocabulary` (50 skills). Generator: `js/modules/gen-vocabulary.js` (573 lines) over the card database `js/modules/data-vocabulary.js` (727 cards). Routing bypasses the category switch: `js/modules/generate-question.js` :310-317 sends any id starting `vocab_grade_` straight to `generateVocabularyQuestion`. Print delegation: `js/modules/print-generate.js` :3732-3761. Machine-readable tags and verdicts: `design/catalogue/vocabulary.overrides.json`.

## Family summary

1. Verdicts: keep 0, fix 38, redo 12, merge 0, split 0 (total 50). All 50 get host `chart-table`: a vocabulary page is a full-width word bank over a numbered table of meanings, and every skill is already `wide` in `SKILL_PRINT_SIZE` (`data.js` :1922-1947). The catalogue's guesses are wrong for all 50 — `visual-grid` 42, `k-counting` 1, `unassigned` 7 — because 603 of the 727 cards (83%) are `modelType: 'text-example'` and carry no visual at all, so there is nothing for a visual cell to hold.
2. Worst problem 1: **roughly a third of every printed sheet is blank.** `print-settings.js` :1401-1421 copies a fixed field list from the generated question and drops `vocabPairs` and `vocabMode`. The print path then reaches `formatProblemForPrint`'s vocab-match branch (`print-generate.js` :4412) with no pairs and falls through to the stub at :4497-4501, printing the prompt plus the italic line "No vocabulary pairs available." The match variant is weighted 30% and is not suppressed during print (`print-settings.js` :1389 sets `state.gameMode = 'practice'`, so the worksheet-mode guard at `gen-vocabulary.js` :161-163 does not fire). Both baseline sheets I opened show it: item 3 of `print/vocabulary__vocab_grade_3_geometry.png` and item 1 of `print/vocabulary__vocab_grade_K_counting.png`. A page with no question on it cannot have an answer key (PT-KEY-1).
3. Worst problem 2: **print/screen parity is inverted (P-29), and in the harder direction.** There is no print handler for `vocab-mc` or `vocab-tf` — the strings appear nowhere in `print-generate.js` and nowhere in `PRINT_FORMAT_SIZE` (`data.js` :1951-2040). The MC options are carried into the print record but never rendered, so the same item that is a four-button choice on screen prints as "Answer: ______" with no word bank. On the Kindergarten sheet that means a K pupil is asked to spell "counter" from the definition "A small object you move to count." True/False prints the same way: a write-in rule instead of two check boxes (owner ruling: the verb is "Check").
4. Worst problem 3: **the True/False variant produces wrong answer keys.** `_genTrueFalse` (:303-337) builds the false case by taking *another card's* definition and marks the item False whenever the definition string differs. Six word pairs share a word inside one pool: `product-3` / `product-fact` in 3:operations and in the grade-3 pool ("The answer when you multiply." / "The answer in a multiplication problem."), `reflect-6` / `reflection-6` in 6:geometry, and `evaluate-5` / `evaluate-expr`, `positive-6` / `positive-number-6`, `negative-6` / `negative-number-6`, `how-many-more-1` / `how-many-more-kw` in the whole-grade pools. Each produces a statement that is true and keyed False. The same rotation also puts the same word on a page twice with contradictory statements (items 2 and 4 of the K counting baseline are both "Less").
5. Worst problem 4: **silent type mixing on every one of the 50 ids (P-28).** The generator rotates six unrelated item types per item at 30/20/20/10/10/10 (:162-192): a drag-to-match widget, definition-to-word MC, word-to-definition MC, True/False, picture-to-word MC and a "Click ALL words that ..." multi-select. The baselines show three different types in the first four items of a six-item sheet. Consequences: one `SKILL_PRINT_SIZE` value cannot be right for all of them (`wide` wastes a whole page width on a one-line True/False and is needed for the match table); no section, test A/B or facsimile key can hold a single type; and the mix itself varies invisibly by pool, because picture-to-word needs four cards with models (:171) and `vocab_grade_6` has one, so the weights renormalise differently per skill.
6. Worst problem 5: **colour and screen furniture carry meaning and reach paper.** The definition panel is blue (`#e3f2fd` / `#1565c0`, :269), the word panel orange (`#fff3e0` / `#ff9800`, :292) and the True/False panel purple (`#7b1fa2`, :327-330) — the panel colour *is* the item type, and it prints in colour against the black-and-white ruling. The `_speakerHtml` TTS button (:44-51) is injected into `q.visual`, so an empty rounded box prints beside every word and every definition on every sheet. Coins are worse: all 8 `svg-coin` cards hit the fallback at :536-541, which reads `data.label || data.value || '¢'` — neither key exists on any card — so penny, nickel, dime and quarter all render as the *same* gold circle containing "¢". Four different answers, one identical picture, in gold currency art the owner has ruled out (PT-VIS-5).
7. Also family-wide: the on-screen prompt repeats the whole panel (`screen/vocabulary__vocab_grade_K_geometry.png` states the 14-word True/False sentence twice, once as the heading and once in the panel); prompts use capitals ("Click ALL ...", :60-119) and the screen verb "Click", which cannot print; 73 of 727 definitions run over 12 words, several over 16; 38 definitions contain the word they define ("Addend: A number you add. In 3 + 4, both 3 and 4 are **addends**."), which is an outright answer leak on the definition-to-word item; the four `svg-number-line` cards render nothing at all because the switch case is `number-line`, not `svg-number-line` (:481), and their `modelData` keys disagree with each other (`min/max/marks` on two cards, `start/end/mark` on the other two) and with the handler (`min/max/highlight`); the eighth `CATEGORY_SETS` entry ("Click ALL even-related words", :116-121) has one `correctIds` entry and is therefore always filtered out by the two-correct rule at :374 — dead code.
8. What is already good: 727 cards with real definitions, grade tags and RIT bands is a substantial asset and the domain split is clean — every grade's card domains map one-to-one onto a skill id, with exactly one orphan (6:integers, 3 cards, no skill); the pool falls back safely when a domain is thin (:136-141); the printed match table at `print-generate.js` :4408-4496 is a correct, deterministic, seeded two-column match with lettered blanks and is the right cell for this family once the payload reaches it; no emoji anywhere (`emoji: 0` on all 50); definitions are written in plain present-tense English with concrete examples, which is the raw material for the `Say:` frames; and `vocab-tooltip.js` already marks vocabulary words in other families' prompts, so a shared term list exists.
9. Existing worked-solution text is not usable. `workedMax` is 2 and `solStepsMax` 3 on all 50, and the text is the hint restated ("Read the definition carefully and pick the word that fits.", :273) — it describes the response format, not the word. `workedSteps`, `wrongAnswer`, the `Say:` frame and the vocabulary terms must be authored for all 50. Because the item is the same shape everywhere, `workedSteps` are authored once per *task* (match / choose / write / judge) and the per-skill authoring is `wrongAnswer` and the three terms.
10. Four ladders are proposed below (VC-R receptive, VC-P productive, VC-C contrasts, VC-S spiral). The receptive rungs exist today as random variants and become ordered options; the productive rungs (write the word from a bank, then with a first-letter cue, then unaided) do not exist at all, which is why the family currently has no item a pupil can fail for the right reason. Three new ids are listed under "New skills needed". No skill in this family belongs to no ladder.

## Proposed ladders

One delta per step. "opt" = an option on an existing id, set per section, never random. Every step below runs on all 43 domain ids; the 7 whole-grade ids are the VC-S2 pool.

### VC-R: receptive word knowledge (K-6, the spine)

| Step | I Can ... | Delta | Skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| VC-R1 | Match a Word to Its Picture (3 pairs) | representation: picture beside word | opt `task=picture-match, pairs=3` (needs cards with models) |
