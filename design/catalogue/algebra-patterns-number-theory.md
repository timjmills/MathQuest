Categories: `patterns` (15), `algebra` (23), `order_of_operations` (12), `number_theory` (14), `algebra_mixed` (7), `all_mixed` (9). 80 skills. Generators: `js/modules/gen-algebraic.js` (`generateOrderOfOpsQuestion` :7, `generatePatternsQuestion` :1012, `generateAlgebraQuestion` :5100) and `js/modules/gen-number-theory.js` (:10). Routing: `js/modules/generate-question.js` :150-190, :345-361. Machine-readable tags and verdicts: `design/catalogue/algebra-patterns-number-theory.overrides.json`.

## Family summary

1. Verdicts: keep 21, fix 32, split 16, merge 7, redo 4 (total 80). The 21 keeps are all review pools (`mixed_*`, `*_all`, `grade_*_mixed`), which inherit whatever their members do. Hosts: `equation-drill` 41, `visual-grid` 18, `chart-table` 12, `word-problems` 9, `computation-grid` 0, `long-division` 0, `k-counting` 0. The catalogue's host guess is wrong for 45 of the 80: `long-division` for `custom_mixed` and `grade_3/4/6_mixed`, `word-problems` for the three `factor_tchart_*` ids, `computation-grid` for six mixed pools, `unassigned` for `compare_expressions`, and `visual-grid` for most of the equation and sequence skills.
2. **Worst problem 1: the adaptive engine silently swaps the skill id.** `adaptive-engine.js:229-231` does `state.skill = ladder[lvl]` for six families, five of which are in this family: `oop` (:55-59), `function_table` (:61-65), `factor_tchart` (:67-71), `factor_links` (:73-77), `gcf` (:79-83). `state.adaptiveModeEnabled` defaults to `true` (`state.js:173`), contradicting the module's own comment that it is opt-in (`adaptive-engine.js:8`). The printed baseline proves it: `order_of_operations__oop_easy.png` is titled "Two Ops, No Parens" and every one of its items is labelled "OoO Medium: Parentheses" with parentheses in the expression; `number_theory__factor_links_hard.png` prints the factor bank that defines the *easy* level ("Bank: 15, 5, 3, 4, 6, 2, 1, 30, 8, 10, 7"). Worksheet and quiz flows are exempted (:216-217) but practice, screen and this catalogue are not. Until this is fixed no page in these 13 skills can be trusted to hold one ladder step, so no test A/B pair, no facsimile key and no footprint is safe.
3. **Worst problem 2: answer leaks on the printed page.** `gcf_easy` prints both factor lists with the common factors already highlighted in amber (`gen-number-theory.js:930-940`, "common factors get gold highlight"), so "pick the greatest one" is reading the largest highlighted box (`number_theory__gcf_easy.png`). `lcm` has an `easy_filled` variant that shows the LCM in green in both multiple strips (:1146-1150). `number_pattern` prints the rule over the first gap — "18 → 21 (+3)" — above the sequence whose rule is the question (:2440-2446). `shape_pattern` prints a "Shape names:" legend under every item pairing each colour swatch with its word, so the typed answer can be copied from the legend.
4. **Worst problem 3: silent type mixing (P-28) on nearly every non-pool skill.** 26 ids rotate through 2-4 unrelated response types per item. The pattern is a `Math.random() < 0.25`-to-`0.35` branch in front of a skill: multi-select on `oop_easy` (:32), `oop_medium` (:127), `write_expression` (:5953), `evaluate_expression` (:6017), `evaluate_expression_hard` (:6155), `solve_unknown` (:5866), `prime_composite` (:43, :74), `multiples` (:746), `gcf_easy` (:881), `gcf_hard` (:989), `lcm` (:1088); drag-to-order or drag-to-bin on `seq_2/5/10`, `count_by_fill`, `number_pattern` (:1100), `pattern_relationship` (:1142), `solve_eq_addsub` (:5423), `solve_eq_multdiv` (:5544), `inequalities` (:6277, :6321), `compare_expressions` (:795). On top of that `prime_composite` picks one of three problem types (:100), `multiples` one of three (:774), `solve_eq_addsub` one of four unknown positions plus a random letter from `x/n/y/a` (:5477-5481), and `divisibility_sort` a random divisor from 2-12 per item (:1239-1243). The drag branches also print as an empty ruled line: in `patterns__count_by_fill.png` three of six cells read only "Drag the numbers in counting order." with no numbers and no answer — an unanswerable, unkeyable page.
5. **Worst problem 4: the count-by skills never count by their own number.** `generatePatternsQuestion` :1014 sets `const start = rng(1, range)`, so "Count by 2s" generates `59, ___, 63, 65`, "Count by 5s" generates `11, 16, 21, ___` and "Count by 10s" generates `54, 64, ___, 84`. The multiples of 2, of 5 and of 10 — the point of 2.NBT.2 — are never on the page. `count_by_step_up` :2116 makes the multiple-of-step start a 50% coin flip and lets values exceed `state.range` (`maxEnd = max(50, range * 2)`; the baseline shows 176 and 178 at range 100).
6. **Worst problem 5: numbers dwarf the step being taught (P-12).** `multi_ops_no_paren` emits six operations with four-digit dividends (`1540 ÷ 44 + 2 × 12 + 72 − 84 + 26`); `exponents_simple` squares two-digit numbers (`34² − 65 = 1091`); `paren_simple` gives `29 × (24 + 38) = 1798`, so the order-of-operations decision is 3% of the work and long multiplication is the rest; `balance_addsub` is tagged grade 3 but generates `3 + 56 = ___ + 54` from `rng(2, 99)` (:5816-5821). None of these is an order-of-operations or equality difficulty.
7. Also family-wide: colour carries meaning everywhere (cyan tiles / orange arrows / green blanks on every sequence visual; per-pair hues on the factor rainbow, `gen-number-theory.js:613`; cyan A column / green B column on `pattern_relationship`; `COLORS.correct` green dot on the inequality line). The `shape_pattern` code states the problem itself at :2305: "each shape needs a distinct color so the student can tell them apart (color encodes the pattern element)" — fill equals stroke, so in black and white every token is the same solid blob. Emoji appear in four visual headers (🔍 :1047, 🔢 :1489, 🔤 :5934, :6382). Tape-diagram bars print as solid black blocks with reversed white numerals (`algebra__tape_diagram.png`) — an ink hog whose digits vanish on a photocopy.
8. Text and response load. Word-problem pronouns do not match the name: `gen-algebraic.js:6446-6448` hard-code "He"/"She" per template against a mixed name pool, and :6527 always says "She … she" for a pool containing James, Ethan, Liam and Owen ("Owen had 85 cookies. She lost 54…"). The T-chart and factor-link prompts run to 24 words and describe a screen interaction on paper: "Type pairs into the rows in any order — both cells turn green when a pair is correct" (`gen-number-theory.js:314`). `shape_pattern`, `number_pattern`, `count_by_fill`, `multiples` and `skip_count_line` all take a typed comma-separated list in one box ("12, 24, 36, 48, 108, 132, 144") where the printed page shows separate boxes — a P-13 and a P-29 break at once.
9. Two outright content bugs. `combine_like_terms` :5215-5225: the 30% "How many x-terms" variant hard-codes `q.ans = 2` and always builds `ax + by + cx`, so every instance of that branch has the answer 2 and needs no reading. `gcf_easy` and `gcf_hard` draw from overlapping pair pools (`[24,36]`, `[30,45]`, `[36,48]`, `[18,30]` are in both, :912, :1019) and produce indistinguishable items; the same is true of `factor_tchart_easy` vs `_hard` (:308 vs :427) and `factor_links_easy` vs `_hard` (:472 vs :603). The real delta in all three trios is whether a factor bank is offered, which is a scaffold level, not a skill.
10. What is already good, and reusable. The order-of-operations generators build a genuine `q.oooSteps` array of 2-4 imperative lines per item ("First: 4 × 3 = 12", "Then: 5 + 12 = 17") — the catalogue's `workedMax` of 9-12 for the OoO skills is the highest in the app, and these lines are a usable draft for `workedSteps` after they are rewritten as imperatives. The printed "Show your work / Rewrite the expression after each step" frame in `order_of_operations__oop_easy.png` is already the playbook's funnel; it only needs to move from every cell to the instruction line. `solve_eq_addsub` already rotates the four unknown positions through an LRU picker (`window.pickVariant`, :5479) and `multi_step_word` rotates four archetypes (:6485) — the right mechanism, applied at the wrong grain (it must pick a step, not shuffle inside a page). `factors_identify` shows all factor pairs with at most four blanks and handles the perfect-square pair correctly (:248-252). Division in every OoO branch is forced to come out whole. `lcm` and `gcf` pools are hand-curated, not random. `tape_diagram` bars are drawn to scale. The playbook ladders OO, EE, PF and NT (`design/EXTENSION_PLAYBOOK.md` 4.13-4.16) fit this family and are adopted below.

## Proposed ladders

One delta per step. "opt" = an option or `responseScope` on an existing id, not a new id. Misconception ids (OO-M, EE-M, PF-M, NT-M) are the playbook's.
