## Family summary

1. Verdicts: keep 6, fix 42, redo 5, merge 2, split 11 (total 66). Hosts after review: `equation-drill` 24, `visual-grid` 17, `k-counting` 12, `chart-table` 13. The catalogue's guesses are wrong for 34 ids — it sends every K counting and comparing cell to `visual-grid`, calls `ten_frame_build` `word-problems` and `base10_build` `equation-drill`, and leaves the nine drag ids `unassigned`.
2. Worst problem 1: **`number_sense:estimate_quotient` does not generate its own skill.** It is missing from `skillCategoryOverride` (`generate-question.js` :284-290 lists the other six estimation ids), so `number_sense` → `rounding` and the id falls through `generateRoundingQuestion`'s final `else` (:3055-3083) with `decimals['estimate_quotient']` undefined → `places = 1`. Every one of the 12 sampled questions is "Round 94.14 to the nearest tenth". The Estimate Quotients code at :4723-4781 is never reached. A pupil who picks Estimate Quotients gets decimal rounding.
3. Worst problem 2: **answers printed on the item.** `placevalue:identify` prints a place-name strip under every numeral with the answer in a black box — all 6 items on the baseline page are pre-answered (`gen-algebraic.js` :3609-3614). `placevalue:value` prints "The 7 is in the hundreds place" and "7 × 100 = ?" (:3593-3597), leaving a one-digit multiplication. `composing:odd_even` captions every option tile "ends in 6" / "ends in 0" (the rule applied for the pupil) and prints "All circles are paired!" under the counters (:1304). `number_sense:nearest_10`'s bar-graph visual labels both distances and says "Shorter bar = Closer = Round to that number!" (:2634-2657); its box visual prints "If ones digit is 0-4: round down" (:2671). `composing:number_bonds`, `make_ten`, `teen_compose`, `base10_build`, `pv_disks_build` and `make_a_ten` all state the full decomposition in the prompt, the hint or the caption.
4. Worst problem 3: **blank or unanswerable print.** All eight `round_sort_*` ids print six identical lines reading "Write each value into the bin it rounds to (nearest ten)." with no tiles, no bins and a single answer rule — nothing to answer and no key to make. `counting:count_objects`'s multi-select branch prints "Circle ALL groups that show 3." above five empty check boxes with no groups drawn. `composing:base10_regroup` prints "Build the number 80 by drawing base-10 blocks in each zone" — identical to `base10_build`, with no regrouping anywhere on the page.
5. Worst problem 4: **silent type mixing (P-28) on 23 ids.** A `Math.random() < 0.25` or `< 0.30` multi-select branch sits in front of 13 ids (`gen-counting.js` :13, :273, :410; `gen-algebraic.js` :3092, :3250, :3364, :3002, :4352-4355, :4786, :4884, :4987). On top of that: `odd_even` rolls three types 40/30/30 (:1259-1263); `estimate_sums_diffs`, `estimate_products`, `estimate_quotient` and `doubles_near_doubles` each roll three (:4568, :4655, :4724, :4917); `make_a_ten` rolls two (:4819); `number_bonds`, `make_ten`, `teen_compose`, `number_word_form`, `place_value_disks`, `place_value_10x`, `compare` rotate two or three variants through `pickVariant`; `rounding_visual` and the six `nearest_*` ids chain a multi-select gate and a number-line-placement gate before the real item. The `nearest_10` baseline page shows the consequence: seven items under three different section headings, two of them with clipped visuals.
6. Worst problem 5: **`state.range` is ignored or misused across rounding and place value.** `makeWhole` (:2692-2712) sets `minN = place + 1`, `maxN = max(place * 2, place * 10 - 1)` and never reads `range`, so `nearest_million` emits 7-digit numbers at Max Number 10 and `nearest_10` never leaves 11-99. `_genRoundSort` (:2504-2512) hard-codes its multipliers. Going the other way, `expand`/`combine` reach 9,999,999 at range 10000 in a grade 2 skill (:3620), `place_value_disks` unlocks millions at range 5000 in a grade 2 skill (:3857), and `number_word_form` (grade 2) emits 4-digit numbers at range 10000 (:3310). `more_less_10` at range 10 computes `rng(10, min(10, 99))` and always returns 10.
7. Also family-wide: colour carries meaning on 44 of the 66 ids — place-value disks and charts are coloured one hue per place (:3836-3843, :3669, :4252-4260), number bonds use purple whole / orange unknown / green known, groups A and B in `compare_groups` are told apart by fill colour, `classify_count`'s "Color" category draws items in the colour that is their name. Emoji survive on four ids (`count_objects` :16, `classify_count` :413-417, `estimate_sum` :4389 📏, `estimate_diff` :4426 📏) against the owner's ruling. Screen wording leaks onto paper ("Click ALL", "Drag the marker", "Drag counters from the palette into the cells", "then check your answer"), and prompts run to 20-27 words on the nine drag ids.
8. What is already good: `count_sequence` is the only skill in the family already drawn in black and white (:146-149), and its number-path cell is the right cell. `expand` and `combine` print cleanly and are the two best-formed items in `placevalue`. `number_seq_fill` has a real chart cell with a sensible blank-placement guard (:2057-2074). Every rounding generator carries an "already rounded" guard so no item is trivial (:2702, :2837, :2904). `place_value_disks`, `pv_disks_build` and `pv_digit_drag` scale their layout as places grow. The `pickVariant` LRU helper already exists and is the mechanism a per-section `variant` option should reuse.
9. Existing worked-solution text is the hint, one or two lines long (catalogue `workedMax` 2 on 44 ids, 3 on the rest). On most ids the hint *is* the method and must be demoted, not promoted: it is currently printed on practice items. It is usable as the key line for `expand`, `combine`, `value` and the six `nearest_*`; everywhere else `workedSteps` must be authored. `wrongAnswer`, the `Say:` frame and vocabulary must be authored for all 60 non-mixed ids.
10. No reference-site research file exists for this family. The open questions it must answer are listed at the end of "New skills needed".

## Questions for the owner

1. **`estimate_quotient` routing.** The id generates decimal rounding, not quotient estimation, because it is missing from `skillCategoryOverride`. Recommendation: add `'estimate_quotient': 'estimation'` at `generate-question.js` :290 as the first change of the migration, before any redesign work on the id — it is a one-line fix and the skill is currently unusable.
2. **The multi-select branches.** Thirteen ids in this family carry a 25-30% "Click ALL ..." branch that emits a different response type, a different print format and, on `count_objects`, an unanswerable page. Recommendation: remove every random multi-select gate from the base ids. Where checking several items is genuinely the skill (`select_even_odd`, the round sorts), it lives on its own id or on the hands-sort page.
3. **Teen numbers 11 and 12.** `teen_compose` and `ten_frame_build_teen` include 11, 12 and 20 in the "ten and some more" step. Recommendation: restrict those steps to 13-19, give 11 and 12 their own step (`teen_eleven_twelve`) because their names carry no "-teen" cue, and cap the teen build at 19.
4. **`hundreds_chart_fill` → `number_seq_fill`.** Same chart, same cell, one blank instead of several. Recommendation: merge as an alias with `blanks=1`; both ids and both share codes stay.
5. **`number_word_names` → `number_word_form`.** Same content at a different size with a bank instead of a written answer. Recommendation: merge as an alias with `task=to_words, response=choice`; the grade 4 six- and seven-digit range becomes the distinguishing option.
6. **Word-form marking.** `to_words` compares free text to an exact string, so "forty two", "forty-two" and "Forty-Two" are not all accepted, and "four hundred and six" is always wrong. Recommendation: normalise case, hyphens, commas and a leading "and" before comparing, and state in the vocabulary band that US convention omits "and".
7. **Does `state.range` bound rounding?** `makeWhole` ignores it, so `nearest_million` produces 7-digit numbers at Max Number 10 and `nearest_10` never exceeds 99. Recommendation (consistent with the 2026-09-19 band ruling, which makes a band an **answer** cap — here the rounded value, P-35): the place sets the band (a nearest-100 item is always at least 3 digits) and Max Number caps the top of that band; when Max Number is below the place, the dialog says the skill needs a larger range rather than silently ignoring the setting.
8. **Grade tags.** `number_seq_fill`, `mixed_counting`, `order_least_to_greatest` and `order_greatest_to_least` are tagged "M" but span K to grade 5 content; `expand`, `combine`, `place_value_disks` and `number_word_form` are tagged grade 2 but reach seven digits when Max Number is raised. Recommendation: keep the tags and bound the content by the ladder step's option instead, so a grade 2 page cannot emit a seven-digit number whatever the global setting.
9. **"Reasonable?" items.** In `estimate_sums_diffs`, `estimate_products` and `estimate_quotient` the proposed answer is always wrong, so "Yes, reasonable" is never correct. Recommendation: generate a correct-and-reasonable answer about half the time; until then, withhold those items from tests, because a pupil can score full marks by never choosing "Yes".
10. **Today's Number breadth.** The catalogue tags 30 of these 66 ids `todays-number`. Recommendation: keep the tag on count, compare, place-value, more/less and round ids (as listed in the overrides file) and drop it from the estimation, sorting and attribute-comparison ids, where there is no single number for the page to be about.
