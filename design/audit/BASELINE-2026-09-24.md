## Systemic (fixed once, in P7)

- No page frame: no strand tab, no "I Can" title, no instruction line, "Maths Quest Pro" footer.
- The answer key is always a list page (H10); `add_wp_10` prints none at all.
- Pagination: short last pages with a mid-page footer (H5); spacious items one per page, page 1 blank.
- Colour, fills and non-Andika fonts on paper and in every screen cell (H4); black-slab fills.
- Doubled answer slots on paper ("Answer: ___" under a visual that has its own slot) and on screen
  (a "Type your answer" field beside the cell's slots) (H8).
- Visuals under the §11.2 minimums (objects 1-5 mm, 7 pt chart numerals, 6 mm bonds).
- Screen: Hint / Read / Skip 36 px tall, worksheet chrome 22-28 px (H6); digits far below 40/48/56 px;
  prompt shown twice; interactive build skills lose their task on the worksheet and quiz (H3).
