Touch numerals 0–9: plain beside touch, at every size the app uses

Each pair: the plain Andika digit, then the touch numeral that replaces it. Rows "trace": the faded-hint variant (marks in the one grey; photocopy: dashed outline). Sizes below 24 pt / 40 px are not drawn: a cell with touch dots is raised to at least that size. Only operands carry marks, never the answer or a sign.
