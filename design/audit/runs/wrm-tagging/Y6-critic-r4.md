## 2. Independent merge simulation (`tools/msim4.mjs`)

I wrote this from the `wrm.js` header. It shares no code with `build.py` or the lane's `mergecheck.mjs`.

**How it reads the data.**
- In `SKILL_WRM`, a string or `{step, note}` is full and `{step, partial}` is partial. Duplicate live entries are flagged.
- The tagFixes are applied in file order:
  - `add` creates the tag, as partial when it carries `partial`;
  - `full` makes the tag full;
  - `partial` sets the clause (R and Y1 keep the clause in `why`);
  - `remove` drops the tag;
  - `opts` keeps the status.

**What it checks.**
- Every key is live and every step exists.
- No pair is touched twice, within a file or across files.
- Each action is legal against the before-state: no `add` on an existing tag, no no-op `full` or `partial`, no `remove`, `opts` or `full` on an untagged pair.
- The why agrees with the before-state ("tagged full", "tagged partial", "not tagged yet").
- After the merge, every direct is full and every partial is partial with the file's clause (several clauses joined with "; ").
- Nothing else stays tagged to a step of the file.
- Every verdict is consistent with its direct, partial and build lists.
