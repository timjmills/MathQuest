## 6. Misconceptions

For answer keys, `wrongAnswer`, error analysis, True or False? and Reason It (P-TH-13).

- **M1** writes the part, not the side length, above the rectangle — e.g. 1564 / 34: writes 1360 and 204 above, quotient 1564
- **M2** drops the place value of the tens part's side length — e.g. 1360 / 34 = 4, so 4 + 6 = 10
- **M3** multiplies the side lengths instead of adding them — e.g. 40 x 6 = 240
- **M4** divides by one digit of the divisor only — e.g. 1360 / 34 -> 1360 / 4 = 340
