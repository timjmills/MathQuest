# Spec card: The four fact drills: + − × ÷ facts on the fact template, fact rows 5 to 10 columns, and ÷ facts in three notations

Generated by `node tests/scripts/ws-spec-card.mjs --family facts` from `design/cards/facts/card.json` and the repo. Do not edit by hand: edit card.json and regenerate. Builders read this card; critics keep reading the full standards and RUBRIC.

Family card for the four fact drills. Each deals one two-number fact per boxed cell from a fact set (a constant, the band, the table) and prints it on the kit `fact` template on every page type; on the fact-rows page (5 to 10 columns) every fact is redrawn at the column ladder's digit. div_facts has five written forms (`divForm`): Standard, Long division, Fraction, Vertical and Mix; on fact rows all three notations - the ÷ sign, the fraction and the bracket - reach the full 10 columns (owner ruling 2026-10-30).

## 1. What it teaches

Recall the basic facts: addition and subtraction within 20 (1.OA.6, 2.OA.2), multiplication and division within 100 / 144 (3.OA.7). A fact is recalled, not worked: no regroup boxes, no work rows, one answer. "Facts to N" bounds the ANSWER for + (the sum) and the number started from for − (the minuend); the × / ÷ band is the largest product or dividend (10 x 10 or 12 x 12).

## 2. Problem types and progression

*From repo sources: PAGE_TYPES 4.1 (fact rows, PT-FRW-*) and 4.2 (fact probe), WORKSHEET_DESIGN_STANDARD §10 (VA-1..VA-5, VA-60..VA-66, VA-70, VA-71), TY-22 / TY-30 (the fact ladder), design/catalogue/{addition,subtraction,multiplication,division}.md and the div_facts forms rounds (critic R1 / R2). Reference sites (MathWorksheets4Kids timed fact drills, Math-Drills 100-fact pages, IXL fact fluency) deal 50-100 facts a page in vertical or horizontal form; our density is the fact-rows table, never theirs.*

**Problem types**

- vertical fact (+ − ×, and ÷ stacked): two numbers, the operator in its own track, the 1.5 pt rule, the open answer row — the core item
- horizontal fact ("8 + 7 = __"): its own section, at most 4 columns (VA-71)
- ÷ fact in its written form (div_facts `divForm`): Standard across, Long division (the bracket, quotient above), Fraction (the dividend over the divisor, = __), Vertical (stacked), Mix (one third each of Standard, Long division and Fraction)
- missing-number facts belong to the cloze / unknown skills, not these drills

**Progression** (one new thing per step, P-1)

1. a single constant ("Add 3", "Multiply by 4") before a mixed set; 0 and 1 facts as their own constants
2. the band grows: + / − facts to 10, then to 20; × / ÷ to 10 x 10, then 12 x 12
3. the fact drills move from the Independent page (5 x 5 at S, 4 x 5 at M, 3 x 4 at L) to fact rows (10 / 6 / 5 columns at S / M / L) as recall becomes fluent
4. ÷: one written form a page (Standard first, then the bracket and the fraction), Mix last

- Standard ("12 ÷ 3 = __") prints stacked on fact rows: the across sentence of 144 ÷ 12 is 28 mm at 16 pt and cannot reach 5 columns at the rung (PT-FRW-7).

## 6. Misconceptions

For answer keys, `wrongAnswer`, error analysis, True or False? and Reason It (P-TH-13).

- **M1** subtracting the smaller digit from the larger whatever the order (13 − 5 read as 5 − 3 in the ones) — e.g. 12
- **M2** counting on / back from the wrong number (starting the count on the first number: 8 + 3 = 10) — e.g. off by one
- **M3** a neighbouring multiple (7 x 8 = 54, 6 x 9 = 56) or the sum for the product — e.g. 56 for 6 x 9
- **M4** ÷ written the other way round: the divisor divided by the dividend, or the bracket read as dividend outside (12 ) 3) — e.g. 0 or 4 for 3 ÷ 12 vs 12 ÷ 3
