## 6. Misconceptions

For answer keys, `wrongAnswer`, error analysis, True or False? and Reason It (P-TH-13).

- **changed-ones** changed the ones digit instead of the tens (43 + 10 = 44) — e.g. a ± 1
- **changed-tens** changed the tens digit instead of the hundreds (367 + 100 = 377) — e.g. a ± 10
- **wrong-direction** added when the sign says take away — e.g. a ∓ step
- **subtracted-for-whole** SU-M7: subtracts when the unknown is the minuend (__ − 4 = 9 → 5) — e.g. |c − b|
- **added-the-numbers** adds the two numbers shown (7 + __ = 15 → 22) — e.g. known + c
- **multiplied-the-numbers** multiplies the two numbers shown (__ × 6 = 42 → 252) — e.g. b × c
- **left-out-zero** M-D2: skips the place the divisor does not go into (721 ÷ 7 = 13) — e.g. quotient with its inner / trailing 0 dropped
- From the build list (entries hosted on addition / subtraction / division): jumps to the wrong ten; forgets the second jump; counts the start number; adds instead of taking away; adds the zeros; 30 + 40 = 700; adds only two of the three; always adds left to right even when a ten is hidden; 37 + 73 = 100; makes 100 in each place (37 + 73); 180 + 30 = 1,110; forgets the hundreds change; adds the two amounts; counts the larger bar; divides by the sum of factors; uses a non-factor pair; = means "the answer comes next"; 7 = 8 − 1 is false because the answer is on the left; subtracts the wrong numbers; checks with the same operation; writes the remainder as a decimal digit (r 3 = .3); fraction over the quotient; everything written; no regrouping check; compensates the wrong way; loses a place; writes the total first (5 + 3 = 2); uses + for a take-away picture; unequal shares; counts the groups not the share; adds the tens as ones; exchanges but forgets to add the new ten; 70 − 30 = 4; 34 + 20 = 36 (adds to the ones)
