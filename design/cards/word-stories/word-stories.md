## 2. Problem types and progression

*No premium logins this round. **Problem types**

- change / join (forms 'change' on add_word_problems): 'Leo had 8 cars. Leo got some more. Now Leo has 13 cars. How many cars did Leo get?' -> 13 − 8 = 5, answer 5 cars. Told in the past tense, kept as told (never retold as 'wants / needs').
- change / separate (forms 'change' on sub_word_problems): 'Ana had 15 shells. Ana gave some away. Now Ana has 9 shells. How many shells did Ana give away?' -> 15 − 9 = 6.
- compare, fewer (forms 'fewer'): 'Maya has 14 shells. Tom has 6 fewer shells than Maya. How many shells does Tom have?' -> 14 − 6 = 8 (consistent language).
- compare, more than, bigger known (forms 'more-bigger-known', the trap): 'Maya has 14 shells. Maya has 6 more shells than Tom. How many shells does Tom have?' -> 14 − 6 = 8 (inconsistent language, P-WP-3).
- Each with the bar on (teaching pages always; practice by option) and off; _plain twins the same stories with no picture row.

**Progression** (one new thing per step, P-1)

1. P-WP-2: one new unknown position a step: join result (exists) -> join change (new) -> separate change (new) -> compare fewer (new) -> compare more-than with the bigger known (new, the trap, P-WP-3 Level 2 up).
2. O2 number size: band 10 -> 20 (G1 lesson) -> 100 (G2 lesson); the band bounds the ANSWER and every number in the story (the start / the bigger amount).
3. O3 support: bar with the known numbers traced (guided) -> bar with blank boxes (independent, option on) -> no bar (option off). The bar frame and its printed labels are structural (RP-71); the traced numbers are the hint that fades.
4. One change a step (P-1): a new value changes the unknown position only; the cell, the sentence and the answer place stay.
