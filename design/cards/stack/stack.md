# Spec card: Stacked arithmetic (the `stack` template)

Generated by `node tests/scripts/ws-spec-card.mjs --template stack` from `design/cards/stack/card.json` and the repo. Do not edit by hand: edit card.json and regenerate. Builders read this card; critics keep reading the full standards and RUBRIC.

One stacked problem per boxed cell: digit tracks right-aligned under place-value heads, the operator in its own track, a 1.5 pt rule, an answer zone below it, regroup / carry boxes above the top number when the scaffold is on. Paper, key and the three screen hosts are one drawing.

## 1. What it teaches

The column (standard) algorithm for addition and subtraction of whole numbers, with and without regrouping, from two-digit numbers to four-digit numbers (three and four addends; across zeros), plus a missing digit in a stacked sum, difference or 1-digit product, and multiplying a multi-digit number by a 1-digit number in the column. Grades 1-4 (CCSS 1.NBT.C.4, 2.NBT.B.5-7, 3.NBT.A.2, 4.NBT.B.4-5; WRM Y2-Y5 column methods).

## 2. Problem types and progression

*From repo sources (PEDAGOGY_STANDARD L-3 to L-5Z, design/PROBLEM_TYPES.md AS / MU rows, design/catalogue/addition.md and subtraction.md); MW4K/IXL research pending logins (Q-H4).*

**Problem types**

- AS-05 vertical add / subtract with labelled columns (H T O letters, regroup boxes above, answer zone below) — the core item
- AS-10 mixed regroup / no-regroup set (P-10 non-examples) and AS-14 mixed-sign set (ring the sign first)
- AS-07 column sum of three or four addends, ragged lengths (`add_column_multi`)
- AS-16 / MU-15 missing digit in a stacked + − × (one dashed digit box, LS-8)
- AS-09 regroup notation only (cross out, write one less, write the new ones); then across zeros (L-5Z)
- AS-11 / MU-11 rewrite a horizontal problem in the grid (alignment), then solve
- AS-15 check a subtraction by adding (`check` line under the stack)
- MU-09 multi-digit × 1-digit in the grid with a carry row (2 × 2 digits and up belong to `long-multiplication`)

**Progression** (one new thing per step, P-1)

1. 2-digit + and − with no regrouping, T O letters, answer boxes (L-3 1-4)
2. the T O letters fade; the grid stays (L-3 7); add or subtract a 1-digit number, ones under ones (L-3 8)
3. decide: does this one regroup? (check box, no computing) (L-4 2, L-5 2)
4. regroup notation only: the ten into the box / cross out and write one less (L-4 3, L-5 4)
5. 2-digit with regrouping, boxes on every item, no-regroup items mixed in (L-4 4, L-5 5)
6. 3-digit: regroup the ones, then the tens, then twice; the answer that needs a new place (L-4 7-10, L-5 10-12)
7. 4-digit with a comma in the answer; three and four addends (L-4 11-12, L-5 13)
8. the regroup boxes fade on a dedicated step only (L-4 13, L-5 6)
9. subtracting across zeros, one change per step: whole ten, hundred, thousand, one zero, two zeros, zeros in the middle (L-5Z Z1-Z7)
10. missing digit (one, then two), check by the inverse, and × 1-digit with a carry row

## 3. The drawing

Template `stack` — `js/modules/sheet/cells/stack.js`; also `fact.js` delegates to it for stacked + facts with a regroup row on paper (`drawsStack`); `screen-cell.js` draws the same cell on the practice card, online worksheet and quiz.

**Sizes S / M / L:** digits 16 / 22 / 28 pt; writing height Hw 6 / 8 / 10 mm; answer row 8 / 10 / 12 mm; heads row 4 / 5 / 6 mm; regroup row 6 / 7 / 8 mm; carry box 5 / 6 / 7 mm; subtraction headroom 8 / 10 / 12 mm (`js/modules/sheet/tokens.js` SIZES). Tracks 0.72 em plain, 0.95 em with any regroup scaffold (TY-20, TY-21); default size L.

- Payload (`q.cell = {template: 'stack', v: 1, payload}`): `operands` (2 to 4 rows), `op`, `ans`, `regroup` ('add' | 'sub' | false), `heads`, `answer`, `unknown` {row, index}, `ansDigits`, `check`, `supports`. The scaffold ladder (level 3-0) decides heads, regroup and answer boxes, never the generator (SCC-Q10).
- States blank / traced / answered / wrong share one geometry (SCC-T10). On a key and a traced Model the regroup boxes hold the working and the regrouped top digits are crossed (AK-2 over VA-13).
- Screen: one input per answer track in the printed geometry, regroup boxes as ungraded inputs, the missing digit as an input (SCC-T15, SP-23).

### 4a. Core rules — apply every one (15 rules; ✱ = quoted in full, the rest cut at a sentence)

**The stack (WORKSHEET_DESIGN_STANDARD §10.1-10.3, quoted in full)**

- **VA-1** ✱ (WDS) Track count: add / subtract d-digit operands T = d + 1; multiply m-digit by 1-digit T = m + 1; multiply m by n (n >= 2) T = m + n + 1; a unit sign adds 1; each separator adds a 0.3 em track.
- **VA-2** ✱ (WDS) **Operator track**: the leftmost track. The operator (700) sits on the bottom operand's row only, and never moves right to sit beside a shorter operand. The top operand's operator track is empty (the label sits above it).
- **VA-4** ✱ (WDS) **Answer row**: 8 / 10 / 12 mm tall (design value; never below Hw), open space with no lines or boxes on paper. An answer track exists under **every** track including the operator track, so a carry-out digit is written under the operator and the layout never reveals whether one occurs. In Guided cells the answer row shows grey 1 pt ticks, 2 mm long, at each track boundary.
- **VA-7** ✱ (WDS) **Missing-digit items**: when a digit of an operand or of the printed answer is unknown, its track holds a missing-digit box (section 6: dashed, track - 1 mm x Hw) in place of the glyph, on the digit's own row. One unknown digit per item at first, then two. Every other digit of the stack prints as usual, the answer row prints its given digits at working size, and regroup or carry boxes, when on, stay solid (LS-8).
- **VA-10@WDS** ✱ (WDS) **Carry box**: one segment of the regroup strip (SL-12): solid 0.75 pt, slot radius at the strip's ends, width = one track, height 6 / 7 / 8 mm, in a regroup row of 6 / 7 / 8 mm. One above **every column except the ones**, whether or not that column regroups, so the boxes reveal nothing. Black in Model, grey 1 pt in Guided, and present as black structural boxes in Independent when "regroup boxes" is on.
- **VA-11@WDS** ✱ (WDS) **Ones box**: in Model and Guided cells the answer row is one answer strip with a segment per track (SL-12: 0.75 pt, one track x 1.2 Hw). The first Model cell links the ones answer box and the carry box above the tens with a dotted arrow pair (hint) to show "write the ones here, regroup the ten there". Independent cells have an open answer row.
- **VA-12@WDS** ✱ (WDS) Any regroup row forces 0.95 em tracks (TY-21). Strip segments are therefore one 0.95 em track wide: 5.4 / 7.4 / 9.4 mm (the old separate boxes were track - 1 mm, 4.4 / 6.4 / 8.4 mm).
- **VA-13@WDS** ✱ (WDS) Regroup boxes are scratch space: never scored, never marked on screen, never auto-focused (SP-23). **On an answer key AK-2 wins** (owner ruling 2026-09-25): the key FILLS the regroup and carry boxes with the full working - the new value of every regrouped top digit (the 5 over a crossed 6, the 17 over a crossed 7) with the old digit crossed out (VA-23), and every carry over the column that receives it. The boxes stay unscored; only the key writes in them.
- **VA-13a** ✱ (WDS) A subtraction's ONES regroup box holds a two-digit number after a regroup (12, 17), so it is two digits wide (1.5 tracks), overhanging the ones column to the right; the other regroup boxes keep one track (lessons r1, H9).
- **VA-20** ✱ (WDS) When the subtraction regroup scaffold is on, a **headroom row of 8 / 10 / 12 mm** is always present above the top operand (structural). It is open space at S and M.
- **VA-22** ✱ (WDS) **Across zeros**: where the top number contains a run of zeros, one wide box spans the run together with the non-zero digit to its left (the pupil renames 40 tens as 39 tens), and a single box stands over the ones. Box layout is derived from the top number's digits only, never from the answer.
- **VA-23** ✱ (WDS) The pupil crosses out given digits by hand; the sheet never pre-prints a strike in Independent cells. Model cells show the strike as a 0.75 pt solid diagonal and the new value in grey.

**Key, tracks and screen regroup boxes (quoted in full)**

- **AK-2** ✱ (WDS) Answers are Andika **700**, black, at the slot's working size, placed exactly where a pupil would write. Working is shown where the page teaches it: regroup digits in carry boxes (on EVERY key - practice, mixed, independent - over VA-13's scratch rule, owner ruling 2026-09-25), partial products, division work rows, quotient and remainder, hands drawn on clocks, shaded parts shaded, plotted points plotted, rings and check marks drawn at 1.5 pt.
- **TY-21** ✱ (WDS) Any regroup scaffold (carry boxes, Tens / Ones boxes, subtraction headroom) forces **0.95 em** tracks in both looks, because a pupil cannot write in a box narrower than about 4.4 mm.
- **SP-23** ✱ (WDS) Regroup boxes are reachable by tap or the Up arrow, are never auto-focused, never block submission, and are **never marked** right or wrong.

## 6. Misconceptions

For answer keys, `wrongAnswer`, error analysis, True or False? and Reason It (P-TH-13).

- **M1** forgets the regrouped ten — e.g. 47 + 38 = 75
- **M2** writes the whole column total, no regroup — e.g. 47 + 38 = 715
- **M3** takes the smaller digit from the larger in each column — e.g. 52 − 38 = 26
- **M4** regroups the ones but does not make the tens one less — e.g. 52 − 38 = 24
- **M5** across zeros: regroups the hundreds but leaves the tens at ten — e.g. 400 − 157 = 353
- **M6** ragged lengths: lines the 1-digit number up under the tens — e.g. 34 + 5 = 84
- **M7** × 1-digit: adds the carry before multiplying — e.g. 23 × 4 = 122
- **M8** missing digit: ignores the regrouped ten (answer off by one) — e.g. 3? + 46 = 82 → 5
- From the build list (entries hosted on addition / subtraction): subtracts the wrong numbers; checks with the same operation; everything written; no regrouping check; adds the tens as ones; exchanges but forgets to add the new ten
