## 2. Problem types and progression

*From repo sources (design/audit/VISUAL_QUALITY.md c-research section 1-2: the US fact-triangle convention, the K-2 story number sentence; PEDAGOGY_STANDARD FF-6, P-10; design/PROBLEM_TYPES.md AS rows). MW4K / IXL logins not used this round (budget); the owner's sample workbooks outrank them.*

**Problem types**

- family-check: three numbers in a triangle, the largest at the top; circle Yes if the two small numbers make the big one, No if not (non-examples: a near miss by 1 or 2, a number bigger than the whole)
- family-check + familyChart: the same item with a blank four-sentence family chart to fill in while deciding (working space; the key fills it for a true family)
- sentence layout: an add or take-away story within 10 / 20; write the two numbers, the sign in the circle and the answer with its unit (the bar drawn on teaching pages)
- _plain story skills: the same stories with no picture row; the sentence and the answer place unchanged

**Progression** (one new thing per step, P-1)

1. family-check with the chart on (the pupil writes the facts, then decides) -> the chart off (decide by adding the two small numbers)
2. stories within 10 with pictures -> within 10 plain -> within 20 with pictures -> within 20 plain (one change a step, P-1)
3. the part-whole bar on the model, guided and lesson pages; on practice pages only when the teacher turns it on
