Touch dots · S1 specimen (approved 2026-09-25, with the owner's rulings applied)

MathQuest's own counting dots, fitted to the default Andika digits with the open 4 (cv04) and nothing else (TY-4). 1 to 5 carry single dots. 6 to 9 carry double dots: a solid dot inside an open ring, touched and counted twice, doubles first. 0 has none. The teacher's word is Touch dots (default) or Count dots. Positions are measured in the browser (ws-touchdots-fit) and held on the strokes by the gate (ws-touchdots). Nothing here is wired into a skill yet.

1 · The digits at 24, 28 and 36 pt, Regular and Bold (size M, keyline on)

2 · Choose the dot size (24 pt, the smallest size allowed)

Andika's stroke is 0.085–0.095 em (Regular), so SF-30's 0.09 em dot is the stroke itself and disappears. Three sizes, each with and without a thin white keyline. The second line of each panel is the same digits through a simulated poor photocopy (toner spread about 0.12 mm, darkened): a double must still show its white gap and its centre dot.

Doubles. Owner ruling: open rings, drawn over the stroke so the numeral's stroke runs into the ring and a 7 or 9 stays whole. The centre dot has its own white keyline (at least 0.26 mm) and the gap keeps the photocopy floor, so after a copy the double is still a ring round a dot, not a blob (gate: ws-touchdots PHOTOCOPY). The knocked-out ring (filled white) is shown for comparison. 28 pt, size M; second line = simulated photocopy.

3 · In real fact cells (kit fact() and stack(), black and white)

Cell sizes follow the kit's fact ladder: L = 5 columns, 28 pt; M = 6 columns, 24 pt; S = 10 columns, 16 pt. Touch dots need 24 pt or more on paper. Owner ruling: when touch dots are on and an item's digits would fall under 24 pt, that item switches to size L (never the dot tile). So at M the column stack (22 pt) prints at L's 28 pt, as shown; at S every touch-dot item prints at L.

4 · On screen: practice card at 1280 px and 390 px

The whole number is one tap target of at least 44 × 44 px. A tap counts the nearest dot not yet counted and turns it grey: black = still to touch, grey = counted (a double takes two taps: the first greys its centre dot, the second its ring). Nothing grows or moves. The count is shown quietly under the cell. "Start again" clears. No timer. Keyboard: Tab to a number, Space counts the next dot in order. Below, at 390 px: a fresh card; 7 × 6 after five taps (counting by 6); 3 + 5 after five taps (count all).
