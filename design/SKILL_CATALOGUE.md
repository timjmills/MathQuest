# Skill catalogue: what every skill supplies today

Generated by `node tests/scripts/ws-catalogue.cjs` (12 seeded questions per skill, Max Number 100, no decimals). Do not edit by hand.

Readiness is measured against the role groups in `design/PAGE_TYPES.md` section 10:

- **ready**: the skill already supplies what the role reads.
- **basic**: the default adapter has material to work from (worked-solution text, answer options, numeric answer), so the page prints, but the content is thin until the family is migrated.
- **author**: nothing to adapt from; the member has to be written in the family migration.
- **wrap**: not a story skill; the word-problem role prints its cell inside the band or a neutral story frame.
- **n/a**: fact layouts apply to fact-like skills only.
- **broken**: the generator threw or returned nothing in this run.

**Host layout** (exactly one per skill) is the practice grid the skill's cell naturally sits in. **Specialised pages** are offered only to skills that carry the tag.
