Two measures, and only the second is the owner's question ("are they fully covered by our current skills?"):

- **Tagged** — at least one live skill is tagged to the code without `approx` (the old measure, kept for continuity).
- **Full** — the audit (2026-09-25) read the tagged skills' generators, options and seeded items and judged them
  against every clause of the standard: **FULL** (every clause is taught and assessed), **PARTIAL** (the missing
  clauses are named) or **GAP** (nothing teaches it). Every missing clause names the proposal that closes it.
  A parent standard with lettered parts is FULL only when its own clause and every part are FULL.

`node tests/scripts/ws-standards.cjs --strict` fails while anything below is not FULL. It fails today by design;
the default run (structure only) stays green.
