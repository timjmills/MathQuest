## Defects (each one causes a score below 8)

**D1. P5: the Say band prints the answer** (RUBRIC C2 "nothing given away"; LESSONS_LEARNED "answer given away").
- The story asks "How many apples?" and the slot is "There are ___ apples."
- The Say band directly below reads "One number is even. So **28** is ___."
- The product the pupil must find, 4 × 7 = 28, is printed on the page. The ☐ × ☐ = ☐ line and the /1 score mark
  nothing the pupil worked out.
- T5 §9 step 3 says the same line aloud, which is fine there because it comes after the answer.
- **Fix:**
  - Make the frame generic, for example "One number is even. So the product is ___." or "___ is even. So the product is
    even."
  - Or give the Say band a blank for the product: "___ × ___ = ___. It is ___."
  - Add a gate: no answer of a page's items may appear in that page's Say band, on any pupil page.

**D2. P6: the exit Say frame coaches the Critical Atom item** (precedent: lesson 3a r1 D4; V22 exit "no help").
- The frame reads "b. 11 × 4: 4 is even. So 44 is ___."
- Item b is the Critical Atom check (T2: "Guided 2, Check Question, exit b"). The frame names the item, its odd row, its
  even column and the box to shade.
- T5 §10 says "No help", but the page gives the help itself.
- **Fix:** use the generic line "An even row or column: the product is even." (the Remember line), or a frame with no
  numbers from an item.

**D3. P6 (and T5 §7): the exit and the Check Question are not what the build claims** (V22 exit freshness; E31 "nothing
unmodelled is assessed"; P-1).
- **Exit b and c share boxes.** b is 11–12 × 4–6 and c is 11–12 × 2–4. Both hold 11 × 4 = 44 and 12 × 4 = 48, so two of
  the three items repeat the same two boxes. SELF-CHECK E24's "a number never twice on a page" does not hold for
  windows.
- **Two of the three items rest on headers never modelled.** The headers 11 and 12 appear on no chart, Guided or
  Practice window before the exit.
- **The Check Question does not use rows 11 and 12.** The brief says it does, but T5 §7 shows 2, 3 × 4–7. None of its 8
  boxes is fresh:
  - row 3 × 4–7 is P3 Independent b's first row, shaded minutes earlier;
  - 2 × 4, 2 × 5 and 2 × 6, 2 × 7 are in P4-1 c and a.
- **Fix:**
  - Put the 11 / 12 rows on the Check Question, so that the board models them before the exit.
  - Give exit b and c disjoint column sets (b 11–12 × 5–7 and c 11–12 × 2–4 share none). Keep exit a on the 2–9 chart.
  - Make the freshness test count shared boxes within the exit page, not only against other pages.

**D4. P6: an empty boxed quadrant on each half** (LESSONS_LEARNED "wasted space"; gold exit layout, lesson 3a P6).
- Three items in a 2 × 2 grid leave the lower-right cell an empty bordered box. It reads as a missing item.
- **Fix:** use the 3a layout. Item a runs full width (or centred), with b | c below. Or set three equal cells in one
  row, if a 2 × 3 window fits at the slip's width.

**D5. P4-1: Practice 1 copies the lesson sheet and overlaps itself** (LR-5 / LR-6; V22 Practice last row = edge cases;
P-1).
- **It copies P3.**
  - Practice a (2–3 × 6–9) is exactly the first two rows of P3 Independent a (2–4 × 6–9).
  - Practice b (3–4 × 4–7) is exactly the first two rows of P3 Independent b (3–5 × 4–7).
  - A pupil can copy the shading from the page before, and the key pattern is identical.
- **It overlaps itself on the one page.**
  - b and d share 4 × 4, 4 × 5 and 4 × 6, plus the turn-arounds 3 × 4 / 4 × 3 and 3 × 5 / 5 × 3. That is 6 of d's 8
    boxes.
  - e and f share 7 × 5 and 7 × 6.
  - a and f share 3 × 6 / 6 × 3.
  - SELF-CHECK's "no same-page turn-arounds" is therefore not true of windows. The near-twin rule of "more than half the
    boxes" should catch b / d, so the turned boxes are not being counted.
- **The ★★★ last row is not an edge row.**
  - Every Practice window has one even and one odd row, two even and two odd columns, and odd × odd boxes. That holds
    for rows 1–2 as much as for e and f.
  - The "big odd" products (21, 27, 35) are already in a and b.
  - T2 atom 7 ("the last Practice row only"), T5 §8 Watch and the T6 ★★★ row describe a contrast the page does not
    have.
- **Fix:**
  - Deal Practice windows that share no row-segment with P3.
  - Count turned boxes in the same-page near-twin test.
  - Make the last row genuinely different. For example, use the window whose even products have odd tens digits (36,
    56, 72, 54: the "tens digit decides" trap) against one whose odd products have even tens digits (21, 25, 27, 45,
    49, 63, 81).
  - Or relabel the row ★★ and correct T2 / T5 / T6.

**D6. P3: the Guided cells carry no hint, so Guided = Independent** (V22 2184: ★ Guided = "the first step of cell 1
printed in grey (trace it); hint scaffolds in grey in every Guided cell"; PEDAGOGY P-LC-7, scaffold fade; T2b's own "★

  - In both Guided cells, ring the even headers in grey, as on the anchor chart's newest marks.
  - In Guided cell 1, print the first even row's boxes as a dotted / grey trace outline to shade over.
  - The Independent cells stay plain.

**D7. T5 §7 / T2: the Check Question's distractor B is not the misconception it names** (T2 Common mistakes; CQ design).
- B is "8, 10, 12, 14, 18, 15": the right answer plus 15 only.

- T2's "Looks like" row quotes the same list.
- The options are given as product lists, and T5 never says to draw three shaded copies of the window for pupils to

- **Fix:**
  - B = all eight boxes shaded, or every odd × odd box shaded.
  - Add "Draw the window three times on the board; shade A, B, C."
  - Use the fresh 11 / 12 window (D3).

**D8. The cube demonstration disagrees across the teacher pages** (V22 teacher-script consistency; T4 C1 "can the
teacher run it from the page").
- T1 Tip, T2 atom 5 and T2 How to teach all say to build the 2 row and the 4 row ("the 4 row is two 2 rows"). Materials

- T4 instead says:
  - "Build 8 rows of cubes in pairs." It does not say 8 rows of what.
  - "8 × 6 = 48. Make it with cubes." That needs 48 cubes.
  - T5 §7's reteach, "build 9 rows of 6", needs 54.

  - either the chart's 8 row ("8 rows of 6: 4 rows and 4 rows, 24 and 24"), with the teacher set of 60 cubes in
    Materials;

- Also make T4 step 2 concrete: "Build 8 rows of 6 cubes."

## Judgements on the builder's decisions

1. **One Practice page, no Mixed page: accepted.** `patterns:notice_patterns` is a build-list proposal, and a Mixed page
   with nothing of the lesson's own to mix would be filler. The acceptance holds only once D5 is fixed: six windows
   that are actually different.
2. **Exit slip of 3 full-size windows: accepted for content.** Three windows, one per step, is the right measure.
   The layout must not leave an empty cell (D4).
3. **Exit and Check Question on rows 11–12: accepted with conditions.**
   - Why it can be fair for Grade 3 SPED: the products are printed, so no 11 or 12 fact has to be recalled. The task is
     the parity of the headers, and the count by 2 on P0 reaches 12 (11 is "one more than 10").
   - Why it is not fair as built: an unmodelled header on 2 of 3 items is a new thing on the assessment. The fix is for
     the Check Question (or Guided cell 2) to show an 11 / 12 window first, and for exit b and c not to share boxes (D3).
   - The claim that the 2–9 chart was spent is partly self-inflicted. Practice a and b re-deal P3's rows (D5), which uses
     up windows that could have been fresh.
4. **P1 place-value warm-up (earlier content, not parity): accepted.** It matches the gold packs. Number sense is
   spaced retrieval, not the lesson.
5. **P0 atom 1 tested by a count-by-2 row: accepted.** The routine needs the parity of the headers 2–12, and a count to
   24 covers it.
6. **Windows nesting across pages: accepted in principle, not as built.**
   - The 2–9 chart has 36 unordered facts and the pack shows about 130 boxes, so boxes must recur across pages.
   - A Practice window that is verbatim the top of the previous page's window, or one that overlaps a window on its own
     page, is not acceptable (D5).
7. **EE M.EE.3.OA.8 versus the owner's M.EE.3.OA.9: recorded.** It is not graded, and the owner is being asked.
