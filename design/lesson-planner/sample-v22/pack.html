Lesson guidance:
Tip: Big first, every time. Pupils who start with the first number count on 7 from 2: slow, and they lose count. Before every item ask “Which is bigger?” and have them touch it.
Tip: Plus means put together. ELL pupils may read + as “and” or not read it at all. Say “plus” and push two groups of counters together each time you say it.
Watch: Counting the start. The most common mistake: 7 + 2 → “7, 8” → 8. The start number is said, not counted. Fix: tap the 7, say it, then put up one finger per count.
