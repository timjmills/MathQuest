Lesson guidance:
Tip: Ones first, always. Every item starts at the ones. Pupils who start with the tens regroup too late. Point to the ones column before each item.
Watch: Smaller from bigger. The most common mistake: 52 − 39 → 27 (9 − 2 in the ones). The top number is always first. Fix: “Is the top smaller? Then regroup.”

How to teach:

Language. Same phrase per step every time; “trade” before “regroup”.

Flow. 3 routine steps are tagged New (Look, Regroup, Check), so the flow is Standard (Chunked needs 4 or more). CRA: rods and cubes, then the stack.
