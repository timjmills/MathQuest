Tip: No square in the I Do. A square turns into itself. It waits for the last row.

Level 3OperationsTeacher 4
Section 5 · I Do: the anchor chart (10 min)
Watch: Turn slowly. Let them see that no dot leaves the tray.
1
Do:Hold up a tray of 4 rows of 2.
2
Say:“4 rows of 2 is 8.”Note:Step 1 and 2: write 4 × 2 = 8.
3
Do:Turn the tray a quarter way round. Slowly.Note:Step 3.
4
Ask:“How many rows now? How many in each row?”Note:Expected: 2 rows of 4.
5
Say:“2 rows of 4 is 8.”Note:Step 4: write 2 × 4 = 8.
6
Ask:“Did a dot move away?”Note:Expected: no. Step 5: same product.
7
Say:“4 times 2 equals 2 times 4.”Note:The Say frame. Pupils repeat twice.
8
Do:Cover the dots. Point to 4 × 2 = 8 on the chart.Note:One new thing: no dots. Practice 2 and exit b, c.
9
Ask:“So what is 2 × 4?”Note:Expected: 8. Same two numbers, same product.
10
Do:Write 2 × □ = 8 on the board.Note:Expected: 4: the factor that moved.
Section 6 · We Do: Guided Practice (8 min)
Feedback: Fix it at once. Fix: turn the tray again and ask “Same dots?”
1
Say:“Look at the first array. Count the rows.”
2
Ask:“How many rows? How many in each row?”Note:Expected ★: 4 × 3 = 12; turned 3 × 4 = 12.
3
Do:Pupils do the second array on their own.Note:Expected ★: 4 × 5 = 20; turned 5 × 4 = 20.
4
Ask:“Turn it. What is the new fact?”Note:Pupils say it; write it on the board.
5
Say:“Cover the dots of the second array. Read its first fact.”Note:Expected: 4 × 5 = 20; no dots, the same turned fact.

Section 7 · Check Question (3 min)
1
Do:Write 4 × 8 = 32 on the board. No dots.
2
Do:Write three turned facts. A: 8 × 4 = 28 · B: 8 × 4 = 12 · C: 8 × 4 = 32.
3
Ask:“Which turned fact is true? Show fingers.”Note:Correct: C 8 × 4 = 32. A: Recounts: a new product · B: Adds the two factors
Most:Go on to You Do.
About half:Reteach step 5 with the tray, then two more.
Fewer than half:Stop. Whole class: We Do again with a new tray.
Section 8 · You Do: Independent Practice (9 min)
Watch: The last row. Walk to the last row first: Practice 1: 16 (a square), 15 (a 5); Practice 2: 49 (a square), 35 (a 5).
1
Do:Pupils finish the lesson sheet, then Practice 1 and 2.
2
Say:“Write the fact. Then say the turned fact.”
3
Do:Circulate at ★★. Look for pupils who recount.Note:Ask “Did a dot move away?”
Section 9 · Word problem (5 min)
1
Do:Read the story aloud two times. Pupils tick each box.
2
Ask:“How many rows? How many in each row?”Note:Expected: 6 rows, 7 in each.
3
Ask:“How many eggs? Say both facts.”Note:Expected: 42; said: 6 × 7 = 42 and 7 × 6 = 42.
Section 10 · Plenary and exit slip (4 min)
1
Say:“Turning does not change the total.”Note:Say it together twice.
2
Do:Hand out the exit slip. No help. Then sort the slips.Note:a. 3, 2, 6 · b. 27 · c. 6.
