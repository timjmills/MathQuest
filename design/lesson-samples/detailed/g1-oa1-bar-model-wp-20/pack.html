Lesson guidance:
Tip: Parts below, whole on top. Say it each time you point at the bar.
Tip: Read it two times. ELL pupils need the first read for the words, the second for the numbers.
Watch: Whole in a part box. The mistake to stop today: the sum written under the bar. Touch the top box: “whole”.

Rows tagged New: 2 → Flow: Standard. One chunk: the two story numbers are the parts; the whole is missing, so add.
How to teach:
  1. Cubes first: two trains of cubes on the bar mat, one under each part box.
  2. Push the trains together under the top box, then draw the bar.
  3. Write the parts before solving: the bar is filled from the bottom up.
  4. Do not bold or hunt key words: point at the box the question asks for.

Section 5 · I Do: the anchor chart (10 min)
Watch: Whole in a part box. Model it wrong once, say “Wrong”, fix it. Never print it.
1
Do:Put the anchor chart up. Read the story two times.
2
Say:“Find the two parts.”Note:Step 1. Point at each number; lay a cube train for each.
3
Say:“Parts below.”Note:Step 2: write each part in a lower box.
4
Say:“The whole is missing. Add.”Note:Step 3. Push the cubes together under the top box. Count on.
5
Say:“Wrong. 12 is the whole. The whole goes on top.”Note:First model it wrong: write 12 in a lower box. Then move it.
6
Ask:“How many stamps in all?”Note:Step 4. Expected: 12 stamps.
7

8
Say:“Gets more: a part. Now: the whole.”Note:Parts 8 + 3; whole 11.
9
Ask:“Swap the cube trains. Same whole?”Note:Expected: yes, the bar is the same length.
Section 6 · We Do: Guided Practice (8 min)
Feedback: Fix it at once. Fix: touch the top box and ask “Is that a part?”
1

2
Ask:“Which box is the whole?”Note:Pupils point at the top box. Expected: 16 cards.
3
Do:Pairs do cell 2: A talks, B points. Check every sheet.Note:Expected: 9 + 8, whole 17 marbles.
4
Say:“Parts below. Whole on top. Add the parts.”Note:The chant. Say it twice together.

Accepted answers:
  • The two parts in either order in the lower boxes.
  • The equation with the two parts in either order.
  • The label word is printed: never marked. On P5, any two-part bar, the whole on top.
  • The answer box and the top box hold the same number. A blank part box with the right whole: ask for the parts.
  • P5 equation: the two parts in either order, + in the circle, then the whole after =.
  • Equal parts (★★★): the two part boxes hold the same number.
  • Exit slip: sort by the boxes ticked; “one more day” pupils join the cube group next lesson.
