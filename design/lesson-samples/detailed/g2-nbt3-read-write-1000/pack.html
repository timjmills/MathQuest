Tip: Keep the chart up. Keep the H T O place chart on the board all lesson.

What students do:
Rows tagged New: 4 → Flow: Chunked. Chunk 1: numeral to expanded form. Chunk 2: words and expanded form back to the numeral.
How to teach:
  1. Draw the H T O chart first, every time: three boxes, so three digits.
  2. Cover part of a number word with your hand and read it in two parts.
  3. Teach numeral to expanded form first: it has the lowest writing load.
  4. Point to each digit and ask what it is worth before writing its part.
Board: teacher models here
Common mistakes:
MistakeBroken stepLooks likeFix

Gives the 0 its placeStep 3305 = 300 + 10 + 5No tens: the tens part is 0, not 10.

Drops the 0Step 335 for three hundred fiveFill the H T O chart: 3 boxes, 3 digits.
