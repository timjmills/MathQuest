I Can find 10 times and one tenth
Level 4Place ValueTeacher 1
CCSS 4.NBT.A.1 · EE M.EE.5.NBT.1 · CRA: Pictorial → Abstract · Flow: Standard (2 new routine steps)
Lesson guidance:
Tip: Values first. The two digits look the same. Their values do not: write both values first.
Tip: One tenth. Say “one tenth of”: 70 is one tenth of 700. Ten tenths make one whole.
Watch: The other way. The mistake to stop today: “10 times” for every pair, whichever is bigger.

How to teach:

Section 1 · Prerequisite Check (5 min)
Tip: Route before teaching. Mark it while pupils do the Number sense page.
1
Do:Hand out the Prerequisite Check, face down.
2
Say:“Do these on your own. Try every one.”
3
Do:Walk and mark as pupils finish.Note:Expected: a. hundreds · b. ones · c. 800 · d. 4000 · e. 50 · f. 760 · g. 2 · h. 87. Routing is on the last page (T6).
Section 2 · Number sense (5 min)
1
Do:Hand out the Number sense page.
2
Say:“Band A: read each row. Write the answer.”
3
Ask:“What is 10 more than 516?”Note:Expected: 526. Band B: the same five kinds, harder numbers.
Section 3 · Spaced retrieval (3 min)
1
Do:Pupils hold mini whiteboards. Write each question on the board.
2
Ask:“10 × 6 = ?”Note:Expected: 60.
3
Ask:“9,344 = ___ thousands ___ hundreds ___ tens ___ ones”Note:Expected: 9 thousands 3 hundreds 4 tens 4 ones.
4
Ask:“Which is bigger: 518 or 544?”Note:Expected: 544.
Section 4 · Chaining bridge (2 min)
1
Say:“You know what a digit is worth.”
2
Say:“Today: the same digit in two places. Which is worth more?”

Section 5 · I Do: the anchor chart (10 min)
Watch: Same digit. Pupils who say “they are the same” are looking at digits. Ask for values.
1
Do:Show 10 rods next to 1 flat. They match.Note:10 tens is 1 hundred.
2
Do:Put anchor chart up. Point to 195 and 10.
3
Say:“The 1 in 195 is in the hundreds place.”Note:Step 1: worth 100.
4
Say:“The 1 in 10 is in the tens place.”Note:Step 1 again: worth 10.
5
Say:“100 is 10 times 10.”Note:Step 2: circle 10 times.
6
Say:“Now the other way: 5 is one tenth of 50.”Note:Another example. Circle one tenth of.
7
Say:“Any two places side by side: the same rule.”Note:Point along the Th H T O heads.
Section 6 · We Do: Guided Practice (8 min)
Feedback: Fix it at once. Fix: cover one row. “What is this 9 worth?”
1
Say:“Look at 9,515 and 2,968. Step 1 is in grey.”
2
Ask:“What is each 9 worth?”Note:Expected ★: 9,000, 900; circle 10 times.
3
Do:Pairs do cell 2: 830 and 8,377.Note:Expected ★: 30 and 300, one tenth of.
4
Ask:“Cell 2: is the top value bigger or smaller?”Note:Smaller, so one tenth of.
5
Do:Pupils do the Independent Practice. Walk and check.Note:Expected ★★: 40, 4, 10 times.
6
Say:“Left is ten times. Right is one tenth.”Note:The chant. Say it twice together.

Section 7 · Check Question (3 min)
1
Do:Write on the board: The 7 in 2,748 is ___ the 7 in 6,570. A: one tenth of · B: the same as · C: 100 times · D: 10 times.
2
Ask:“Which words fit the blank? Show fingers.”Note:Correct: D 10 times. Each wrong option is one T2 mistake: A: Wrong way round · B: Same digit, same value · C: Counted two places
Most:Go on to You Do.
About half:Reteach with rods and a flat, then two more pairs.
Fewer than half:Stop. Build 10 and 100 with blocks. Count the rods.
Section 8 · You Do: Independent Practice (12 min)
Watch: The last rows. Walk to the last rows first: Practice 1: 349 (one tenth, thousands and hundreds); Practice 2: 3078 (ones and tens, 10 times).
1
Do:Pupils finish the lesson sheet, then Practice 1 and 2.
2
Say:“The Steps are at the top of the page.”
3
Ask:“Which value is bigger?”Note:Visit pupils who circle the same word every time.
4
Do:Practice 2: the same steps, numbers to 99,999.Note:Look for the digit written in a worth box.
Section 9 · Word problem (4 min)
1
Do:Read the story aloud two times. Pupils tick each box.
2
Ask:“What is each 6 worth?”Note:Expected: 6,000 and 600.
3
Ask:“How many times as much?”Note:Expected: 6,000 ÷ 600 = 10.
Section 10 · Plenary and exit slip (4 min)
1
Say:“Left is ten times. Right is one tenth.”Note:Say the chant together twice.
2
Do:Hand out the exit slip. No help. Sort the slips by item b.Note:a. 500, 10 times · b. 40, one tenth of · c. 800, one tenth of.

Accepted answers:
  • A value written with or without its comma (7000 or 7,000) is right.
  • Said aloud: “ten times as much”, “times 10” and “a tenth of” are all right.
  • A right ring with an empty worth box: not yet right; ask “what is it worth?”.
