## Static lint (`node tests/scripts/ws-lint-static.mjs`)

`standard`, `wide`, `spacious` are size words assigned to the wrong field; `conversion`,
`fraction-numberline`, seven `geometry-*`, four `measurement-*`, `mult-chart`, `mult-chart-tier`,
`number-pattern`, `shape-pattern`, `vocab-mc`, `vocab-tf`). 148 have no `PRINT_FORMAT_SIZE` entry
(they fall back to the skill's size, then to `standard`). Every skill has a `SKILL_PRINT_SIZE` entry.
